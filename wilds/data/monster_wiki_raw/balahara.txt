=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Balahara is found primarily in the [[Windward Plains]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Balahara primarily attacks by biting, firing mucus from its mouth, and using its tail like a lance to stab its target. Balahara is also capable of burrowing underground and repeatedly leaping at the hunter before eventually emerging. In certain areas, while underground, it is also capable of creating large pools of quicksand that pull hunters towards its center. If Balahara is hit by a [[Screamer Pod (MHWilds)|Screamer Pod]] while underground, it will be forced out of the ground and into a Toppled state.

Balahara is capable of climbing onto the various Fulgurite pillars to unleash attacks from. The ones present in its nest in Area 15 will weaken when they are climbed on, allowing for them to be collapsed with the Hook Slinger, causing Balahara to fall to the ground for massive damage and a knockdown.

During its mucus spit and mucus spew attacks, Balahara's mouth is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its mouth, Balahara will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however.

While in wait in quicksand, Balahara acts as an environmental trap, collapsing the sand under any (non-Balahara) large monster that passes over. Anything caught in the trap will fall into the Balahara's nest in Areas 14 or 15.
