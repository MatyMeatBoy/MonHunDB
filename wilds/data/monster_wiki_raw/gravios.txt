=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Gravios is found primarily in the [[Oilwell Basin]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. In the Basin, it is most commonly seen during the Inclemency and slightly less often in the Fallow.

Due to its weight, Gravios relies on relatively slow physical attacks, such as charges, body slams and rolls. It is also capable of expelling various gases from its belly: be it fire, poison or sleep. It can also fire off powerful lava lasers from its mouth, either as sustained beams or as smaller missiles.

While its body armour is normally resillient to most attacks, after performing certain attacks smoke will come out of parts of its body, be it the head, belly, back or tail. This signals which parts of its body have become weak points for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on one of these parts, Gravios will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction shared between all parts. By breaking the armour on its chest, it becomes a permanent weakspot for damage. Breaking the armor on its back will prevent it from discharging fire gas after a laser beam.
