=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Uth Duna is found exclusively in the [[Scarlet Forest]], appearing most often during the Downpour, although it can rarely be found during other weather conditions.

Uth Duna primarily uses slow but powerful physical attacks with its claws and belly. With its water veil up, and while submerged in deep waters, these attacks are augmented by waves that push the hunter away from Uth Duna, with the more powerful waves also dealing damage.

Uth Duna begins the fight covered in a veil of water. These veils are considered separate from the monster itself, and do not damage Uth Duna when attacked. However, dealing enough damage can break them individually, weakening Uth Duna's defenses. Destroying enough of them will dispel the rest and briefly knock the monster over, but without the veils weighing them down, its speed and aggression will increase significantly. Uth Duna will restore its veils after enough time has passed without them, damaging and pushing back hunters caught in the resulting explosion.

While in its nest in Area 17, Uth Duna will be able to swim in the deep waters, performing leaps to create especially powerful waves. Several [[Wedge Beetle|Wedge Beetles]] present at the edge of the reservoir allow hunters to avoid them, and a pair of Loch Ruins in the water can be torn down onto Uth Duna when it swims past using the Hook Slinger, damaging the monster considerably and forcing it back into the shallows, where it will be toppled.

After performing its leaping belly flop, Uth Duna's mouth is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its mouth, Uth Duna will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however.

==== Arch-Tempered ====
Arch-Tempered Uth Duna receives the following moveset changes:

* Previous moves have greatly increased mobility and are performed more aggressively.
* Water veils have greatly increased durability.
* Attacks with the water veil active produce more frequent waves that deal more damage and pushback.
* Can perform additional new attacks.
