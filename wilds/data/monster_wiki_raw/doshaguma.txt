=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Doshaguma is found in both the [[Windward Plains]] and the [[Scarlet Forest]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered individuals]] also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. While in the Windward Plains, Doshaguma can be encountered in packs lead by an Alpha Doshaguma, who is a guaranteed Gold Crown Large individual. Doshaguma found in the Forest and Ruins are solitary.

Doshaguma is a purely physical monster, primarily attacking with its claws, by biting, or by slamming its body into the hunter.

After taking enough damage, Doshaguma will rear up on its hindlegs and perform a special roar, causing all previously destroyed wounds to re-open. In this state, Doshaguma is capable of new attacks, can combo the old attacks together in new ways, and extends its ground scrape by digging its claws into the ground and unearthing debris in front of it. Outside of its exhausted state, it will remain enraged for the rest of the fight.

While on its hindlegs before a ground scrape, Doshaguma's belly is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on their belly, Doshaguma will reel back and be briefly knocked down.
