=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Rathalos is found in the [[Scarlet Forest]] and [[Oilwell Basin]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. In the Forest, he will most commonly appear during the Fallow, while in the Basin, he emerges most often during the Inclemency and more rarely in the Fallow.

Rathalos, while grounded, primarily attack by biting and shooting fireballs. However, Rathalos prefers to attack from the sky, lunging at the hunter with his poisonous talons and breathing out streams of fire. Damaging him enough while he is flying causes him to flinch in the air for an extended period of time, flinching him again will knock him out of the sky. [[Flash Pod (MHWilds)|Flash Bombs]] can also be used to instantly knock him out of the sky.

His nest in Area 11 of the Scarlet Forest features piles of decaying plant matter. If one of his flame attacks touches the piles, they will burn for roughly a minute.

After performing any of his fire breathes, Rathalos' mouth is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on his mouth, Rathalos will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
