=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Mizutsune is found primarily in the [[Scarlet Forest]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Mizutsune attacks by sliding across the ground using his bubblefoam, often attacking with his brush-like tail in the process. Hitting Mizutsune while he is sliding around will topple him. Additionally, Mizutsune is capable of shooting both jets of water and bubbles, with some of his attacks also sending off bubbles. Small bubbles inflict Minor Bubbleblight, large bubbles inflict Major Bubbleblight. In addition, he will sometimes produce green and red bubbles, which heal you and boost attack respectively.

While low on health when he enters his enraged state, he will form a blue fiery glow in one of his eyes, similar to [[Soulseer Mizutsune]]. In this state, he can spit fire-element bubbles as well as perform attacks similar to Soulseer.

After performing a water burst, Mizutsune's mouth is left exposed as a weak-point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on his mouth, Mizutsune will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
