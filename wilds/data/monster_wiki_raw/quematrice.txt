=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Quematrice is found primarily in the [[Windward Plains]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered individuals]] also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. 

Quematrice mainly attacks by biting and swinging its tail, spreading flames as it drags it across the ground. By severing the tail, the monster's ability to spread fire with it is removed. The fires spawned from its attacks are capable of setting alight any patches of grass they come in contact with. 

After performing its flaming tail spin, Quematrice's neck is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on the neck, Quematrice will reel back and be briefly knocked down.

They spread a flammable substance which vaporizes quickly, then it ignite it by dragging their tails along the ground. By nature, Quematrice rarely hunt their own prey, and instead scavenge herbivore carrion left behind by other predators. This often leads to confrontations with other, smaller carnivores.</br><br/>'''Helpful Hints:''' Keep an eye on where the flammable material settles on the ground to gauge how much a Quematrice's fire will spread.
