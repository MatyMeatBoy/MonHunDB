=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Gogmazios can only be found in the [[Forgotten Machineworks]]. Its hunt is a three phase battle with drastically different behaviors in each phase, with each phase moving to a new area with numerous hazards that can be leveraged against it. Notably, unlike most hunts, 8 Hunters in total can partake in the quest, with 4 Support Hunters and up to 4 players.

Gogmazios itself has two main battle states pertaining to the status of its oil coating: '''Solid''' and '''Liquid.''' In the Solid state, Gogmazios' hide is significantly toughened and it is resistant to the Dragon element, but can have its hide softened by Fire damage, expediting its melting into the Liquid state. In the Liquid state, Gogmazios' hide will temporarily soften and become vulnerable to Dragon, but the melting oil will constantly slough off of its body as puddles, impairing Hunters on contact and exploding when struck by its physical attacks or other explosions. The Liquid state also loosens the Compact Anti-Wyvern Guns around its body, which can be grabbed using the Claw Slinger and used as Slinger Ammo, with no limit on how many can be grabbed.

In the first phase of the battle, Gogmazios will use powerful physical attacks using its wingarms, head, and tail, along with occasional oil breath. Its attacks spread oil in the surroundings.

When enough damage is dealt, Gogmazios will fire its burning oil beam to turn the entire ground into highly damaging '''Abyssal Lava,''' then crawl to the zone's ledge and lean over it. In this phase, Gogmazios continues using physical attacks, but now uses its burning oil beam, which leaves behind trails and patches of volatile oil that explode violently after a delay. Attacking its wingarms in this phase can imbalance it and cause it to topple, exposing its back as a weak point for Focus Strikes.

Optionally, during this phase, breaking its back twice will dislodge the '''Convergent Elderbreaker''' lodged in its back, after which Hunters can connect it to the Wyrmway using 3 '''Loose Nexuses''' that appear in the arena. When all are connected, it will begin charging up, and can be aimed and fired at Gogmazios to deal a massive amount of damage and topple it. The longer the Elderbreaker is allowed to charge for, the more damage it will deal.

When reaching a certain amount of HP, Gogmazios will unfold its wings and fly to the highest point of the Forgotten Machineworks, prompting the Hunters to pursue. At the top, it no longer enters the Solid state and can enter a new '''Gaseous''' state: its oil coating begins to vaporize, rendering it highly vulnerable to Dragon damage, but Gogmazios attacks incredibly aggressively, constantly sheds burning oil, and can now fly to attack from above with its wingarms and oil beam. Attacking Gogmazios with Compact Anti-Wyvern Guns will make its flight begin to wane, while pressing the attack will make it fall down and render it unable to fly for a time.

During the Gaseous state, Gogmazios will constantly build up power, culminating in a special attack where it first lands and begins causing the ground all around it to rapidly erupt, becoming incredibly vulnerable to Dragon damage in the meantime. It then flies into the air and unleashes a massive barrage of flaming oil while dealing constant burning damage to all Hunters in the zone, culminating in it landing in a massive explosion that causes a near-guaranteed faint. Dealing continuous damage to Gogmazios, most significantly Dragon element damage, will suppress its power, weakening the strength of the next special attack up to two times. If the attack has been weakened at least once and [[Fabius]] is active, he will run to a nearby corner of the zone and raise his shield, creating a safe zone behind him that shields Hunters from the blast, or Hunters with shields can also block it. Hunters can also avoid the attack regardless of strength by climbing up any high ground, but all high ground will be destroyed the first time it uses the attack.

Breaking the Mutated Armaments across its body can provide additional Tarred Devices as rewards. However, they can only be damaged in the Liquid or Gaseous state.
