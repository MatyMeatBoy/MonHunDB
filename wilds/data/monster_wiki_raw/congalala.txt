=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Congalala is found primarily in the [[Scarlet Forest]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered individuals]] occasionally being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. 

Congalala primarily attacks with its claws, by slamming its body down and by farting. While not carrying anything in its tail, it is also capable of excreting into it and throw its dung forward. 

Additionally, Congalala is capable of performing a variety of breath attacks depending on the type of mushroom it has recently eaten. Periodically, Congalala will sniff into the air before going over to a patch of mushrooms and picking one up with its tail, which it will proceed to eat in order to change the effect of its breath. Congalala's breath attacks will inflict Soiled by default. After consuming red [[Nitroshroom (MHWilds)|Nitroshrooms]], its breath will inflict Blastblight; after consuming yellow [[Parashroom (MHWilds)|Parashrooms]], its breath will inflict Paralysis; after consuming purple [[Toadstool (MHWilds)|Toadstools]], its breath will inflict Poison.

Occasionally, Congalala will instead dig into the ground and pull out a black [[Truffle Du Conga (MHWilds)|Truffle Du Conga]]. Consuming this particular mushroom will allow Congalala to use all its breath attacks simultaneously. However, if it is flinched before it can finish eating, it will drop the mushroom which can be picked up as an item and used as a cooking ingredient.

If caught in a [[Pitfall Trap (MHWilds)|Pitfall Trap]], Congalala will fall into it face first, leaving its rear exposed. When enraged, Congalala is capable of farting in retaliation whilst trapped.

After performing a fart, Congalala's rear is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its rear, Congalala will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
