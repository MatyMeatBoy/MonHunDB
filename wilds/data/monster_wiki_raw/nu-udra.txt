=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Nu Udra is found primarily in the [[Oilwell Basin]] and is primarily active in the Firespring, although it can rarely be found during other weather conditions. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]] from MR 100 onwards.

Nu Udra primarily attacks with physical strikes from its six grounded arms, being able to launch attacks in nearly any direction and strike with multiple arms at once. Each arm can be individually severed to reduce their range and provide extra carves.

Once enraged, Nu Udra's body begins glowing as it sets it alight and begins to use more powerful fire-based attacks. This includes flaming oil projectiles that create delayed explosions on impact, and using its siphon to expel large amounts of fire on the ground. Eventually, Nu Udra will shed its oil coating in a dense rain of flaming oil, reapplying its oil coat afterwards. Dealing enough damage to it while it is enraged will knock it out of its enrage, remove the oil coating on its body and toppling it, weakening it for a time.

While in its nest in Area 17, Nu Udra is able to burrow into the cracks in the ground to quickly reposition around the area.

As it does not rely on sight to detect prey, it is immune to [[Flash Pod (MHWilds)|Flash Bombs]] and similar effects.

After performing its quadrupal arm slam, Nu Udra's beak, located behind its face tentacles, is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its beak, Nu Udra will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however.

==== Arch-Tempered ====
Arch-Tempered Nu Udra receives the following moveset changes:

* Previous moves are performed faster and more aggressively, with further reach.
* While ignited, emits a burning aura that deals constant damage to nearby hunters. This can be negated by Level 2 [[Adaptability (MHWilds)|Adaptability]].
* No longer exits the ignited state unless forced out of it via damage.
* Can perform additional new attacks.
