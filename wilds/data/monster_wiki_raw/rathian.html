Summary

Fearsome green Flying Wyverns known and feared as the "Queen of the Land", and the female counterpart of Rathalos. While still capable of flight, they prefer to stay grounded. Ferociously territorial, Rathian fiercely protect their nests from the ground, charging down foes and using their powerful fire breath and the several venomous barbs which cover their tail.

Physiology

The Rathian is an archetypal Flying Wyvern, displaying the common body type of the class: two hind limbs, two large wings consisting of a membrane stretched over bone phalanges, and a long tail; hers ends with a bulbous thorn tip. The Rathian belongs to the same species as the Rathalos and is the female counterpart, she can be distinguished by her green shell, golden eyes, and characteristic bundles of toxic barbs. She sports a longer chin spike in comparison to the Rathalos, one which serves functional purposes. This chin spike is not a solid structure like the Rathalos', and is instead a hollow feeding tube that allows the Rathian to push pre-chewed food through it to provide small pellets of food to her young.[6] As the breeding season approaches, this chin spike grows and is employed as a shovel, which the Rathian uses to dig a hole for her nest.[7]

The Rathian grows characteristic rigid barbs that are arranged in patches on the shoulders, the wingarms, and the tip of the tail. These barbs are extremely toxic and administer the Rathian's signature poison upon contact.[6] These barbs are actually strands of modified hair made rigid and hollow to serve as toxic weapons.[8] While they are largely uniform in length and shape, a handful of barbs on the Rathian grow thicker and sharper, becoming capable of carrying higher doses of venom.[9] The barb patch on the tail allows for venomous tail swipes, while the patches on the shoulders protect the Rathian from airborne assaults.[6]

Another distinguishing feature of the Rathian are her muscular thighs; much more developed than the Rathalos', these muscles allow her to run across the ground with speed and stability.[10] This is believed to be an evolutionary adaptation to the role the Rathian takes during brooding behavior: the Rathian is tasked with providing food for the young, and as hunting on land is more reliable than hunting in the air, the Rathian developed these powerful legs to aid in terrestrial predation.[6] Despite her grounded specialisation and preference, the Rathian still flies solidly. Her impressive wings are adorned in sharp wingtalons along the outermost phalange, easily able to cut down trees and rend boulders to rubble.[11][12] Moderately difficult to work with, these wingtalons serve as a blacksmith's first test,[13] and the wings' heat resistant membrane finds use in armor lining despite their reputation as a legendarily difficult material to work with.[14][15]

Like the Rathalos, the Rathian is capable of spitting fireballs.[6] This ability is fueled by a specialized Flame Sac that produces and stores a flammable powder. By stimulating this sac and exposing this powder to air, the Rathian can produce blasts of fire from her mouth.[16] The Rathian is broadly considered to be more proficient at using her fire in comparison to the Rathalos.[6] Curiously, the bone marrow of both the Rathian and the Rathalos is highly flammable, igniting instantly upon contact with air.[17] The Rathian additionally produces highly sought-after rubies and gems within her body.[18] The Rathian's armor consists of countless green scales overlapping and layering over each other into highly resistant carapace; these scales are known to thicken as the wyvern ages.[19] Very rarely, the Rathian may grow an inverted scale on its tail that points in the wrong direction. Called a Rathian Plate, this scale is searing hot and extremely valuable.[20] While her outer shell is durable, even the Rathian's medulla is notably hard and proves a difficult item to craft when harvested.[21] Should one bypass her carapace, they will gain access to meat that is considered a beloved food and which can be cooked in the form of Rathian Chops.[22]

The Rathian displays a degree of regional variation, specifically between the Old World and the New World. In the New World, the Rathian sports denser barb patches and more flexible wings, as well as a wider wing span on average. Furthermore, it has been observed that the New World population of the Rathian is more proficient at flying compared to that of the Old World.[10]

Behavior

The Rathian is a powerful terrestrial predator and is known far and wide as the "Queen of the Land".[23] A single swipe of the Rathian's claws will easily kill an adult Aptonoth,[24] and she is capable of hunting a pack of Delex, using her fire to blast the dunes they swim through whilst controlling the direction of the group, before then quickly smashing them out of the sand with her venomous tail.[25] The Rathian will mark her territory multiple times a day with claw marks,[10] although does not display extraordinary territorial aggression beyond that.[26] When she is not paired with a male and nurturing young, the Rathian lives apart from and does not share natural habitats with the Rathalos. She will instead congregate with other Rathian in distant regions and live completely without the Rathalos, as one such Rathian colony was observed to the east of Dundorma.[6]

During the breeding season, the Rathian will seek out the Rathalos. Here, the behavior between the Rathian of the Old World and the Rathian of the New World differs greatly. In the Old World, the Rathian and Rathalos will form a monogamous mated pair that equally shares parenting duties.[26] Meanwhile, the New World Rathalos will mate with an entire harem of Rathian, and only the most dominant female will be allowed to raise her offspring with the Rathalos. All the other Rathian are pushed out of the male's territory and take residence in the Wildspire Waste, where they must raise their young on their own.[26] By contrast, the chosen Rathian will enjoy the loyal and ferocious protection of her mated Rathalos.[27] In both cases, the Rathian will end up laying a clutch of 10 eggs into a dug out nest made from rotting pant matter, often located in caves or treetops.[7][26]

Once a mother, the Rathian's demeanor changes significantly. While the wyvern still largely ignores smaller monsters, she will attack relentlessly if she identifies a threat to her nest and will lay waste to any that disturbs her nest, such as troublesome Jaggi.[26][28] If the Rathalos is involved, it is usually his task to protect the territory, while the Rathian's main objective is to secure food.[6] In one instance, due to being in an agitated state as a result of the breeding season, one lucky researcher was able to observe the wyvern facing off against the Kirin in an unusual stand off. In an attempt to intimidate the encroaching Elder Dragon, the Rathian repeatedly roared and spewed her flames, albeit to no avail due to the impervious wall of lightning summoned by the Kirin. Too intense to be called a stand off, yet too cautious to be deemed a battle, neither sought to cross each other's boundaries.[29]

The Rathian is considered highly intelligent, particularly due to some of the behaviors she displays while rearing her young.[31] While the Rathian is extremely protective of her nest, she will allow certain species of small carnivores to live in peace nearby. The species that are spared from the wyvern's wrath are chosen based on their ability to ward off ovivores—such as rats—who would snatch up the Rathian's nutritious eggs. By allowing these carnivorous wyverns to exist, the Rathian ultimately protects her young by proxy.[7][26] In other cases, it was observed that the Rathian may not kill small prey, such as the Velociprey or Aptonoth on the spot, instead choosing to injure and then drag it back to the nest. Once there, the injured prey is presented to the young, which can practice hunting in a safe environment.[31]

Due to her ecological dominance and fascinating social behavior, the Rathian and Rathalos have long since inspired awe among the people.[6] There are even areas—such as the hunting town of Dundorma—in which the pair are worshiped as part of a religious belief.[32]

Super Biology Information

The Super Biology Book Series collects the thoughts and theories of real scientists that were asked to speak about monsters from the Monster Hunter series. Thus, they are inherently speculative and should be seen as such.

Fossil Expert Yoshitsugu Kobayashi notes in his report that the Rathian and Rathalos are likely poorly suited for movement on land: The tibia and femur bones in their legs are the same length, which suggests limited mobility when walking.[33] By comparison, the wings of the two wyverns possess a number of internal bones - similar to bats - which suggests that they are exceptionally good at turning and maneuvering mid-air. Kobayashi also theorizes however that the trade-off here might be that these wings excel at short-distance bursts of agility to hunt prey, but are less suited for long-distance migratory flights. Either way, since the Rathian and Rathalos rely so heavily on their wings, Kobayashi believes that they are unlikely to degenerate over the course of evolution.[34]

Kobayashi notes that both the Rathian and the Rathalos are covered in short, backwards pointing spikes, which he suggests likely evolved as a way to defend against attacks from above and behind. The angle and arrangement of the spikes prioritizes defending the back of the neck, and Kobayashi likens it to structures that were possibly found on Pachycephalosaurus. The spikes could have also served as decoration.[33]

Rathian and Rathalos’ parenting strategy - wherein the female secures food while the male defends the young - reminds Kobayashi of descriptions of Troodontidae. Such a division of labor is known to increase survival rates through specialization of the parents. Kobayashi also notes that this sometimes leads to females of a species being more physically powerful than the males, as it has been proposed to be the case with Tyrannosaurus rex.[35]

The Rathian and Rathalos’ ability to create and expel fire is considered a miracle beyond all known limits of nature, as no real creature has ever been observed having such an ability. Kobayashi asserts that because in traditional biology, internal tissue is known to break down at temperatures nearing or exceeding 45 °C, the Raths must possess special organs made from special cells that are exceptionally heat resistant. The flame production mechanism must require fuel, ignition and expulsion processes, and Kobayashi believes that these three elements are handled by this species’ Flame Sac, which likely sits near the lungs. A specialized one-way valve connects the Sac to the lungs: Fire and fuel is prevented from entering the lungs, but air from the lungs can be pushed into the Sac to catalyze the flame creation and funnel the created fire breath into the throat to be expelled by the mouth.[35]

Development

Monster Hunter

Rathian was originally created and designed by Tomonori Kambe.[36] It was the first monster designed by the development team, which is why she is the monster displayed on the Monster Hunter logo instead of Rathalos. The original concepts were a 'poisonous green dragon'.[37]

Monster Hunter 3

Much like Rathalos, Rathian would be re-designed and overhauled by Yuya Tokuda during the game's production.[38]

Monster Hunter: World

During development, the team had already decided to include Rathalos, so it was obvious that Rathian would appear as well. She is first encountered during the quest The Best Kind of Quest, which according to Yuya Tokuda was done so that players could choose to face her at their own pace. When Rathian quests first become available to the player, the next story-related monsters are Tobi-Kadachi and Jyuratodus, two monsters that require specific approaches to defeat. By comparison, Rathian's attacks are clear and easy to avoid but with heavy consequences for failure. The idea was that players would use Rathian as encouragement to craft some new equipment and prepare for the battles ahead.[10]
