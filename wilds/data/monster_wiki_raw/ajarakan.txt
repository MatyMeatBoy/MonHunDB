=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Ajarakan is found primarily in the [[Oilwell Basin]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered individuals]] also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. Ajarakan is often encountered and travels in pairs.

Ajarakan attacks with its powerful forelimbs, either slamming them forward or scraping them across the ground. While its body is heated, most of its attacks with its arms will additionally fling up blast-inflicting molten debris. 

As it fights, its body gradually heats up, culminating with it digging its arms into the ground and causing its back to catch on fire. While in this state, standing too close to it will cause passive damage, additionally, weaker arrows and Bowgun ammo will fail to deal significant damage. Eventually it will exit this state after performing its spinning ball slam, though it is not guaranteed to. Dealing enough damage to Ajarakan in this state will forcibly knock it out of it and topple it.

Ajarakan is also capable of hanging and swinging from the overhead debris present in certain zones, eventually leaping and slamming towards its target. It can be knocked down while climbing to topple it.

After performing its massive explosion attack while heated, Ajarakan's arms will be left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its arms, Ajarakan will reel back and be briefly knocked down.

|Description='''Characteristics:''' Characteristic by their highly developed carapaces, Ajarakan wrap themselves in molten metal that they rub together to heat up and create explosions. Presumably due to the friction of the metal, they can reach high enough temperatures to melt even the ground or bullets. They can also use their strong limbs to grip onto the ceiling and move around freely, so a hunter must watch out from every angle, including above.</br><br/>'''Helpful Hints:''' Approaching an Ajarakan's extreme heat will sap your health, so use cool drinks or Chillmantle Bugs to counteract it. Normal Ammo and arrows will wilt if you try to pierce their molten carapaces, so you must use heat-resistant equipment or hit them with Watermoss to bring down their temperature first. Continuous friction will cause their body temperature to rise, but soften their outer shells.</br><br/>'''Map Description:''' Fanged beasts that adhere metal to their bodies and use friction to heat and cause explosions.
