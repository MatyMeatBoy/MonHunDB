=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Gypceros is found in the [[Windward Plains]], [[Oilwell Basin]] and [[Iceshard Cliffs]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. In the Plains, it only emerges during the Fallow, while in the Basin, it appears more commonly in the Plenty.

Gypceros attack using its elastic tail which is capable of extending multiple times its normal length. It can also spit out poison that flows with the terrain and drips from ceilings, often doing so while charging. Hitting its poison with a [[Puddle Pod (MHWilds)|Puddle Pod]] will dissolve it harmlessly. Gypceros also possesses a crest capable of producing a blinding flash, instantly stunning any hunter within range. By breaking its head twice, this crest can be removed.

When near death, Gypceros is capable of faking its own dying animation, though no Quest Complete screen will appear. During this state, it can be carved for materials. Eventually, its body will twitch before it flails around back onto its feet.

While in its nest in Area 11 of the Oilwell Basin, Gypceros is capable of running on the walls during its charging spit.

Due to the rubbery properties of its hide, Gypceros is immune to both the Thunder element and to [[Shock Trap (MHWilds)|Shock Traps]], and due to the use of its own blinding flash, it is immune to [[Flash Pod (MHWilds)|Flash Bombs]].

After performing its flashbang, Gypceros' crest is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its crest, Gypceros will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
