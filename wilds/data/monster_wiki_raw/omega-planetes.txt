=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Omega Planetes is found in the [[Rimechain Peak]] and has four phases, transitioning to the next zone of the Peak with each phase. During phase transitions, it can rain down additional missiles.

In the first phase, Omega Planetes uses physical attacks with its legs, arrays of laser beams fired from the cardinal sides of its body, and flame jets from its underbelly. It also fires missiles from its back that land in telegraphed explosions, often while other attacks are being performed. Periodically, it will spawn [[Omega Micros (MHWilds)|Omega Micros]], which chase players and explode. Some of the Omega Micros will be glowing. These ones will spawn a shield if killed. Several of its attacks will also uniquely be announced in the chat log: 

* '''Rocket Punch:''' Creates several mechanical fists that track down targets and explode. Dealing any damage to the fists while they're flying will destroy them. If there is only one hunter in the quest, Omega will summon two fists, both of which will target the one hunter.

Dealing enough damage to Omega Planetes' head will cause the attacker to gain '''Enmity,''' whereupon Omega Planetes will almost exclusively focus on them until it wears off, or another target draws its Enmity. In return, the scope of Omega Planetes' AoE attacks will be greatly limited, now being entirely focused on the target of its Enmity.

In the second phase, Omega Planetes gains several new attacks, including a large laser beam that chases the targeted player which can be guarded or outran. It also gains the ability to enter the '''Pantokrator Mode''', which dramatically increases its aggression and the frequency/number of its attacks. This mode persists for the rest of the phase, but will end prematurely if three [[Wounds (MHWilds)|Wounds]] are made and destroyed on its legs. It also gains new announced attacks:

* '''Mustard Bomb:''' A glowing crosshair tracks the target for several seconds, before Omega Planetes fires a large bomb that leaves behind a long-lasting pool of highly damaging flames. This attack can only be used if Omega Planetes does not have Enmity drawn. This will instantly destroy shields, but if it hits a shield, it will not spawn the flame pool.
* '''MRV Missile:''' Omega Planetes stands still and repeatedly fires barrages of missiles at all Hunters. If a hunter currently has Enmity<u>,</u> the missiles will only target them instead of the party. This attack is only used in the Pantokrator Mode.

In the third phase, Omega Planetes upgrades its attacks and gains several new ones, of note including the following:

* It can summon an energy orb that inflicts HP Penalty when dealing damage, even if guarded. This penalty applies a maximum health reduction and successive orbs can stack this penalty, severely reducing the hunter's total HP.
* '''Starboard/Larboard Wave Cannon:''' After a brief charging time, Omega Planetes pivots 180 degrees, unleashing a barrage of lasers that covers its initial right (Starboard) or left (Larboard) side.

At a certain HP threshold in the third phase, Omega Planetes will spawn the [[Nerscylla Clone]] alongside a large number of Omega Micros, then begin charging the '''Delta Attack''', an enormous beam of energy that will result in a guaranteed cart to all players if it connects. Slaying the Nerscylla Clone before the charge time finishes will create a special shield that will protect against the Delta Attack. Numerous [[Cactaur (MHWilds)|Cactaurs]] and [[Morbol Seedling (MHWilds)|Morbol Seedlings]] will also spawn in the zone, which can obstruct players but may also be used to incapacitate the Nerscylla Clone. If the Delta Attack is not interrupted but the hunt doesn't end, Omega Planetes will permanently become more damaging and resilient for the rest of the fight. 

In the fourth phase, Omega Planetes will enter the Pantokrator Mode again and upgrades its attacks further. At low HP, it enters the Pantokrator Mode a final time and seemingly will not exit it until it is defeated.

After and while firing its frontal beams, large laser, or Wave Cannon, Omega Planetes' head will become a weak point for Focus Strikes. Successfully executing one will cause Omega Planetes to flinch and drop a Shield at its location, which can block most of Omega Planetes' attacks while it persists. A Shield can also be created by destroying glowing blue Omega Micros. 

In the Pantokrator Mode, after using its flailing flamethrower combo, Omega Planetes' legs become weak points for Focus Strikes, successfully executing one causes Omega Planetes to briefly collapse. Upon breaking each leg, Omega Planetes will topple for a prolonged period of time and drop an Omega Fragment, which can be picked up as a Shield Generator and used to create a Shield on demand. 

Omega Planetes takes increased damage from attacks derived from the [[Soul of the Pictomancer]], and will flinch when hit with successive [[Thunder Pod (MHWilds)|Thunder Pods]].

Omega Planetes is immune to Traps, {{Element|Poison|[[Poison]]}}, {{Element|Sleep|[[Sleep]]}}, {{Element|Exhaust|[[Exhaust]]}}, and Flashes.

==== Savage ====
Omega Planetes in the [[Planetes Protocol (Savage) (MHWilds Quest)|Planetes Protocol (Savage)]] quest has the same basic fight structure as the original, but with the following changes:

* Has access to its Phase 3 moveset from the start of the fight, with its moves becoming even more dangerous as the battle progresses.
* Can now utilize jets of freezing mist that inflict {{Element|Frostblight|[[Frostblight]]}}.
* Its stomps now can sometimes produce a cloud of mist that inflicts {{Element|Frostblight|[[Frostblight]]}}.
* Naturally regenerates from Wounds if they are not destroyed in time.
* Upgrades Starboard/Larboard Wave Cannon to '''Starboard/Larboard Wave Cannon Surge''' starting from Phase 3, which has Omega Planetes perform a second Wave Cannon shortly after the first, covering the initially safe side.
