=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Rompopolo is found primarily in the [[Oilwell Basin]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Rompopolo attacks utilising its sharp, blade-like forelimbs. Its posterior sac is capable of creating explosions in the ground that, if they occur on oil silt, create temporary platforms that are easier to walk on. Its anterior sac is capable of expelling a poisonous mist. If this sac is deflated, Rompopolo loses this ability. If Rompopolo's forelimbs are broken, its heavier forelimb attacks will make it briefly stagger or fall over.

When not enraged, the gas sacs on its head, back, and tail can each be individually deflated, toppling the monster if all three sacs are deflated. Eventually however, Rompopolo will re-inflate its sacs. While the monster is enraged, damaging one sac enough will cause all of its sacs to deflate, the monster to be knocked over, and for Rompopolo to exit its enrage.

While performing a poison attack, Rompopolo's tongue is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its tongue, Rompopolo will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however.
