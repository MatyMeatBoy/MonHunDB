=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Seregios is found primarily in the [[Windward Plains]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Seregios attacks primarily with its powerful hind-legs, often flying into the air beforehand to lunge at its target. It is also capable of firing off blade-scales from its head, wings and tail. Breaking a Seregios' parts reduces the number of blade scales it is capable of firing off. Seregios is additionally capable of picking up [[Herbivore|Herbivores]] on the map and tossing them at the hunter.

Some of Seregios' scales, when landing in terrain, can be picked up as Bladescale Pods.

After its sweeping talon attack or divebomb, one of Seregios' legs is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes (either its left for the sweep, or the stuck leg for the divebomb). If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its leg, Seregios will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
