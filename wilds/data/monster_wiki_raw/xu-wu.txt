=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Xu Wu is found primarily in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Xu Wu attacks almost exclusively with its arms, using its mucus to form golden blades on the limb it is about to attack with. It is also capable of throwing these blade-like secretions as projectiles. While its front arms can only be broken, its middle and back arms can be severed to reduce their range.

Occasionally, Xu Wu will rear up, exposing the mouth on its underside before lunging forward at its target. If the monster is flinched while preparing to lunge, it will enter a special toppled state where its mouth is exposed. This will also occur when a hunter hits Xu Wu's mouth with Slinger ammo if it grabs and attempts to devour them. 

While in its nest in Area 12, Xu Wu is capable of burrowing into the ground in order to reposition, including to emerge from the ceiling into an immediate lunge with its mouth.

As it lack any eyes, it is immune to [[Flash Pod (MHWilds)|Flash Bombs]] and similar effects.
