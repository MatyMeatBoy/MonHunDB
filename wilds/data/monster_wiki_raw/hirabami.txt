=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Hirabami is primarily found in the [[Iceshard Cliffs]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered Individuals]] also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. While in the Iceshard Cliffs, Hirabami is often encountered in groups of 3.

Due to the membrane on its neck, Hirabami is capable of floating in mid-air, attacking with its claw-like tail, and by shooting ice shots capable of freezing hunters in place. Breaking its head causes Hirabami to take longer when getting back in the air, and destroying any wounds on either its head or tail will instantly topple the monster. The tail can be broken up to five times, dropping a [[Heavy Slicing Pod (MHWilds)|Heavy Slicing Pod]] each time; hitting its head with this pod will knock it out of the air.

After shooting out ice, Hirabami's mouth will be left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its mouth, Hirabami will reel back and will be knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however.
