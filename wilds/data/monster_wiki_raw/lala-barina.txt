=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Lala Barina is found primarily in the [[Scarlet Forest]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered individuals]] also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. 

Lala Barina attacks with its claws, stabs with its legs and the paralytic stinger normally kept hidden in its abdomen. Lala Barina is also able to unfurl its abdomen, releasing several florets into the air around them; if enough of these florets make contact with a hunter, they will be left paralysed. When Lala Barina enrages, its abdomen will permanently unfurl, causing it to release more florets as it repositions.

While in its nest in Area 4, Lala Barina is capable of hanging from the ceiling by a thread of silk and swing in a circular motion, spreading a large quantity of florets throughout the area, dismounting shortly afterwards.
