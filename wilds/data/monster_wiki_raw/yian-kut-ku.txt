=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Yian Kut-Ku are primarily found in the [[Scarlet Forest]], with [[Frenzy Virus|Frenzied]] individuals also being found in the [[Iceshard Cliffs]]. They can also be found in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. In the Scarlet Forest, they form large packs during the Plenty. Lonesome [[Tempered Monsters|tempered]] individuals can also be found in any season.

Yian Kut-Ku attack by pecking at their target or by spitting a fiery liquid, often doing so while charging forward. Yian Kut-Ku possess a highly acute sense of hearing, meaning that [[Screamer Pod (MHWilds)|Screamer Pods]] are capable of stunning the monster. However, once they have recovered, the Yian Kut-Ku will become instantly enraged. They are immune to the effects of Screamer Pods while enraged.

While engaging with a target, the ears of a Yian Kut-Ku will fan out, however when they are near death they will be folded back up again.

While falling over after a charge, Yian Kut Ku's mouth is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on their mouth, Yian Kut-Ku will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
