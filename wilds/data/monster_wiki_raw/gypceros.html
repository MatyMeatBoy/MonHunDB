The Poison Strange Bird Gypceros (ゲリョス, Geryosu) is a Bird Wyvern introduced in the original Monster Hunter.

Summary

Omnivorous Bird Wyverns that can easily be identified by their distinct Rubbery Hide, which protects their body in the absence of hard shells or scales. A Gypceros is able to produce a blinding flash of light to stun and disorientate opponents through the comb-shaped crest on their head by striking it with the horn on the tip of their beak. They are also capable of expelling vast quantities of viscous poison. Known for feigning death, before ambushing their enemies with a surprise attack.

Physiology

The Gypceros is a strange, medium-sized Bird Wyvern identified by its uniquely rubbery and pliant hide, which protects its body in the absence of developed scale or shell covering.[4] This hide insulates the monster from electric shocks and toxins,[5] and is known for its surprising resilience and odd scent.[6] It is a matte, gray-blue across most of the body, while the underside bears a matte-cream coloration. The tail, wing membranes, and band running down the length of the neck are all rosy-brown, and the breast and underside of the feet possess a gray-brown, ribbed covering.

The skull is short and stout with prominent brow and cheek ridges, as well as a crest composed of mineral ores and crystals, which is formed by swallowing rocks and is attached to a specialized muscle that allows it to be moved.[7] The beak is gently curved and distinctly buck toothed, it is decorated by five horn growths on the chin, and a single large horn with a broad, blunt tip on the snout. By striking its crest against this horn, the monster shatters the minerals, causing a reaction that produces a blinding flash.[7] Rare materials, such as Lightcrystals, can be found within the crest.[8]

When enraged, the cream-toned skin surrounding the Gypceros' eyes will turn a deep-red, and it will begin to salivate the toxin produced within its body.[9] It can spew large quantities as a toxungen[c] from its mouth, and while its toxins are considered less potent than that of other monsters, they are more potent than an Ioprey's. Exposure to large quantities can be dangerous even even prove fatal to Hunters.[8][10] The globs of toxic sludge that the Gypceros spits are adhesive and can cling to terrain for some time, although are harmlessly dissolved in water. Another resource produced within the Gypceros' body is a fluid known as Dash Extract,[8] to which it owes its exceptional stamina.

Its hindlegs are stout and muscular, and the toes exhibit the same arrangement seen in the Yian Kut-Ku, with the first toe facing inward and the remaining three forward. However, its vary more in length by comparison, with the third being almost twice the length of the fourth. This similarity between different species is likely due to both monsters sharing a common ancestor, known as the Borudoru.[11] Owed to its great stamina and powerful gripping hindfeet, the Gypceros is capable of running up and along steep walls and cavern ceilings.

Each wing possesses three fingers that support the wing membranes, along with a stout thumb. Like the rest of the body, the wings are resistant to poison and electricity.[12] The Gypceros has an exceptionally elastic, pink tail that ends in a bulbous shape.[13] When swung as a club, it can stretch to over twice its original length.

Behavior

The Gypceros is considered to be very intelligent in comparison to other wyverns.[7] It has a reputation for curiosity towards items carried by humans an infamous tendency to steal them, with a preference for pilfering shiny objects, such as Gypceros Crystals,[14] as well as various gems and ores. It was theorized that it may simply enjoy collecting things like humans do,[7] however, it was discovered that during breeding seasons, the male Gypceros engages in a very unique courtship ritual. By scattering its shiny collection around its nest and flashing its headcrest when it notices a female flying by, it creates a truly dazzling display of luminous treasure. Should the female approve of the display, she will fly down, securing the male the opportunity to mate. Males have even been witnessed presenting gifts of their treasures to females, with larger, shinier presents earning more appreciation from the females.[15]

When the Gypceros is cornered and its arsenal of defensive tools fails to deter its attacker, the wyvern will play dead to confuse its foe.[8] Should the attacker stick around, the Gypceros will suddenly lash out in an attempt to scare off the opponent. This peculiar behavior is not observed in any other monster outside of its closest relative: the Purple Gypceros.

The Gypceros is omnivorous and has been observed feeding on Vespoids, rotten carcasses, and Toadstools. Upon stealing something edible—such as meat, insects or mushrooms—the monster will consume it immediately.[7]
