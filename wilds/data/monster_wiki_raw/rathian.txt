=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Rathian is found in the [[Windward Plains]], [[Scarlet Forest]] and [[Oilwell Basin]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]]. In the Plains and the Forest, she will most commonly appear during the Plenty.

Rathian primarily attack by charging forward, backflipping into the air with her poisonous tail or by shooting fireballs. While not as adept as her [[Rathalos (MHWilds)|mate]], she is also capable of flying into the air to attack. After breaking her wings, she will occasionally stumble while attemtping to take flight, leaving her on the ground. Severring her tail will remove its ability to inflict poison.

Her nest in Area 11 of the Scarlet Forest features piles of decaying plant matter. If one of her flame attacks touches the piles, they will burn for roughly a minute.

After shooting fireballs, Rathian's mouth is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on her mouth, Rathian will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
