=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Zoh Shia is fought exclusively in the [[Dragontorch Shrine]] located in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]], during Wyvern's Wakening, becoming available for Quest creation in a similar manner to [[Jin Dahaad]]. Zoh Shia cannot be fought in its Low Rank form after the story, except by answering other players' SOS flares.

In its first phase, it will be in its '''Crystalized State''' where its body will be completely covered by armor that makes it more resilient and renders it immune to Wounding, which can be broken to deal more damage to the monster. Any broken armor parts will regenerate after some time has passed, although they will be significantly easier to break after regrowing. Zoh Shia primarily uses physical blows from its wingarms, and infuses the ground with energy explosions that create Wylkrystals, which can be detonated by its elemental attacks. Hunters can detonate these Wylkrystals with elemental Slinger ammo, dealing damage to Zoh Shia if it is close enough. The explosion of the Wylkcrystals can also cause a chain reaction with other nearby Wylkcrystals, knocking it down if enough hit it at once; every 3rd wound destroyed will also knock down Zoh Shia. Dealing enough damage will cause it to reveal its head, which will use fireball and fire breath attacks while exposed. The head can still grow back its armor, but will be forced to shed it when breathing fire.

Zoh Shia begins phase 2 by creating a large number of massive Wylkrystals and igniting the ceiling, causing ignited debris to rain down across the area and periodically fall for the rest of the fight. It then breathes a torrent of fire that drastically intensifies after a short delay and ignites all the Wylkrystals to expand its reach. Hunters can detonate the Wylkrystals early with elemental Slinger ammo to prevent Zoh Shia from using them to spread its flames. In this phase, Zoh Shia is still able to use Wylk attacks, but liberally uses a wider array of fire attacks using its wings, which are now also revealed alongside its head, including jets of flame that leap along the ground, and a smaller version of its phase transition attack.

In the third phase, Zoh Shia completely sheds its armor and loses the ability to grow it back, showing its true form as it enters its '''Fallen State''' and gaining the ability to use red lightning attacks; namely in the form of blasts along the ground and a rain from the sky. It will always begin the phase by creating a large number of Wylkrystals around itself, then sequentially striking them with lightning to create explosions.

Zoh Shia is immune to both traps and [[Flash Pod (MHWilds)|Flash Pods]], as well as any similar effects.

After its Wylk-creating wingarm slams while its arms are exposed or have their armor broken, Zoh Shia's wingarms are left exposed as weak points for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its wing-arms, Zoh Shia will reel back and be briefly knocked down, though there is a cool-down to this interaction.
