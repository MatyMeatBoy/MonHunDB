=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Guardian Doshaguma is found exclusively in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]].

Much like its base counterpart, Guardian Doshaguma primarily attacks both with its claws and by biting. Additionally, its corrosive drool causes its bite attacks to inflict Defense Down, and can be flung at hunters.

Certain attacks performed with Guardian Doshaguma's arms will cause bursts of Wylk to occur, signalled by its arms glowing white beforehand. When these attacks are performed on pools of Wylk, they will leave behind a Wylkrystal. Hitting a Wylkrystal with most forms of elemental attack will cause it to explode, dealing damage corresponding to that element.

Like all Guardians, Guardian Doshaguma is capable of regenerating any wounds inflicted upon it. However, destroying enough wounds will cause the monster to topple and prevent it from channeling Wylk into its attacks. Guardian Doshaguma will re-enter its empowered state upon absorbing enough Wylk from the ground.

After performing a Wylk-based attack with its forelegs, it will be left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its forelegs, Guardian Doshaguma will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down on this interaction however.
