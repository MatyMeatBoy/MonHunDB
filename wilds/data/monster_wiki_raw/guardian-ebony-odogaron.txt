==Hunter's Notes==
{{MonsterDescription
|Description= '''Characteristics:''' An artificial monster species created by an ancient civilization and imbued with Ebony Odogaron's power. Guardian Ebony Odogaron cannot reproduce and do not require food, relying instead on Wyvern Milk for energy. They will attempt to corner targets with flurries of attacks from their well-developed claws and tails.<br/><br/>'''Helpful Hints:''' Guardian Ebony Odogaron are most notable for their honed claws that induce bleeding and dexterous tails, both of which carry Guardian energy that can trigger explosions. If their dragon-element attacks connect with Wylkrystals, it will cause an explosion of the same element. However, since the crystals are triggered through elemental interference Slinger ammo can also be used effectively.</br><br/>'''Map Description:''' An artificial species created by an ancient civilization with an Ebony Odogaron's power. The flurries from their foreclaws pose a threat.
