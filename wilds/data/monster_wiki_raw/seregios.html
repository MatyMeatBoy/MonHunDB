Summary

Ferociously territorial golden Flying Wyverns with uniquely zygodactyl feet bearing razor-sharp talons. The Seregios' most distinct features are the countless, backwards-facing scales that cover its body, which can be dislodged and fired at enemies as lethal projectiles, causing profuse bleeding in anything they hit. They are extremely belligerent, rivaling even a Rathalos in the air, and frequently drive other monsters out of their territories, including their own kind.

Physiology

The Seregios is a species of Flying Wyvern recognizable by the unique blade-like golden scales that cover its entire body. Dark-red stripes accent the sides of its neck, shoulders, wrists, legs, and its singular horn, otherwise the wyvern's coloration is almost entirely a shimmering gold, with a lighter tan underbelly. The beauty of these scales is not their only notable trait; when the Seregios is angered, the scales on its head, back, legs, wings, and tail will stand on end, revealing them to be razor sharp. Due to their sharpness, these scales are commonly referred to as Bladescales.[5] This display is rightfully threatening, as the scales on their own are sharp enough to gouge foes by merely grazing them.[6] The scales of the Seregios are constantly growing and are made of heat-resistant minerals.[7]

Additionally, the Bladescales can be launched by the Seregios and will ride the wind until they embed into targets, making them exceptionally dangerous.[8] The Seregios is alarmingly accurate with this maneuver, able to hit small Remobra out of the sky with individual Bladescales.[9] When the scales make contact with an object or a creature, they will burst into smaller scale fragments that cause heavy bleeding. The Seregios does not appear to have a limit as to how many scales it can dislodge from its body, and as such, it uses them rather freely in combat.[10]

Alongside the knife-like Bladescales, the Seregios' extremely powerful hind legs are remarkably dangerous, partially a result of its zygodactyl feet that are perfect for gripping and slashing. It uses its sharp talons to latch onto its prey,[11] tearing through skin and armor alike.[12] The talons are often used to make weapons,[13] and they are said to be sharp enough to rend the air itself with their lightning-fast strikes,[14] causing prey to bleed profusely.

The Seregios' skull features a singular large horn resembling an axe protruding from its snout,[15] this horn is hefty and sharp, yet conveniently agile, making it a popular material for crafting blades.[16] The horn typically remains partially folded against the monster’s face, standing upright once it grows enraged.[3] The skull additionally features a beak-like maw, a small lone spike protruding from the lower jaw, and dark-orange markings surrounding its blue eyes. The skeletal structure of its unexpectedly tough two-pronged tail is not unlike that of the Rathalos and Rathian's;[13] the thick Bladescales that cover it completely can be flung by the Seregios with violent swings of its tail. The spear-like tail tip may also be used by the Seregios, often to cleanly pierce through the hide of its prey.[17]

Few can match the Seregios' mobility and superiority in the air, it is incredibly fast in flight and capable of making sudden sharp turns and performing various acrobatic stunts to attack and reposition, sometimes both in a single motion. Its wings are each equipped with a sharp wingtalon that helps shift the monster's weight during flight,[18] in turn enabling the Seregios to perform tight turns.[19] Like the talons on its hindlegs, the Seregios' wingtalons are prized as a crafting material.[20] Excluding the talons, the wings feature four fingers that attach to the wing membrane, each of them tipped with a sharp claw; when angered, only the scales on the outermost digit will stand on end. Strangely, the Seregios rests its wingtalons on the ground and uses them when walking, suggesting it may be more closely related to the Tigrex due to its posture.[21] Furthermore, due to its unrivalled flying capabilities and hunting strategy, the Seregios was initially thought to be a Bird Wyvern when it was discovered.[2]

Behavior

Until recently, the Seregios was only known to exist in an extremely remote area and was considered to be incredibly elusive.[22] Due to its inhabited area having limited space, the Seregios would commonly fight over turf with others of its kind, which in turn resulted in a relatively small population as weaker individuals were killed off in these battles.[23] The position of the Seregios' nest is representative of its standing within its territorial hierarchy: the Seregios who nests in higher altitudes is considered a stronger individual, while the weaker Seregios will nest in lower areas.[24] A Seregios that nests high in the region it occupies creates the strongest Bladescale bursts and possesses superior flying capabilities comparatively. Due to this behaviour, the Seregios tends to flock in areas with vertically-stretched terrain, such as mines.[24]

During an outbreak of the Frenzy Virus, one individual was reported to have overcome the virus and become an Apex Seregios. This single Seregios drove the entire rest of the Seregios population out of their native habitat, resulting in the species spreading out into other locales and more frequent sightings.[25] The population has become so widespread that the species has been seen in regions as far as the Forbidden Lands.

The Seregios is fiercely territorial, often attacking other large monsters and frequently competing with the apex predators of the ecosystems it invades in intense combat.[26] When hunting prey or attacking other monsters, the Seregios will make use of its powerful hind legs while launching Bladescales from its body. Once the Seregios' prey has succumbed to its aggressor's brutal assault, the wyvern will use its Bladescales to form a golden grave marker, embedding them into the ground in a circle around its fallen foe's corpse.[13] The Seregios is known to come into conflict with the Rathian for an uncertain reason, these conflicts may end fatally.[9][27]

Development

Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate

Seregios was created by Yuya Tokuda.[28] When Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate entered production, the series had gone many years without a Flying Wyvern Flagship Monster. Additionally, the most recent representatives of the class - Tigrex and Nargacuga - both emphasized a flightless combat style and were in a sense atypical Flying Wyverns. Thus, Tokuda suggested that the Flagship Monster of Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate should be a proper Flying Wyvern that attacks players from the sky. However, merely flying around and attacking from above would not make for a unique enough monster, and so Tokuda created the concept of a Flying Wyvern that attacked using its powerful legs to kick players.[29]

Based on this concept, the design team created prototype designs for the monster based on Secretary Birds.[30] Kaname Fujioka advised them to pay close attention to how they shape the wings of the monster: In order to counteract the image of the Tigrex' muscular forearms, he suggested that the Seregios should have light and delicate wings, with the main muscle mass being reserved for the legs. This would give the Seregios a more distinct look compared to previous Flying Wyvern designs.[29] Fujioka also asked the designers to consider the stance of the monster, and requested that it actually use its wing-arms for light support when grounded, as a reference to its creative ties to Tigrex. Tokuda took this insight to further differentiate the combat style of the Seregios. In order to make it feel appropriately different from Rathalos, he designed the flying style of the Seregios to revolve around it pushing itself off the ground with its powerful legs and then rapidly gliding from place to place using its light wings. This gave the monster a fast and ferocious look during combat, and also made it feel like it could rival the King of the Skies.[31]

Visually, the Seregios was designed to look immediately distinct from the Rathalos and Rathian. Initial concepts for it gave it a blue coloration to contrast against the Rathalos' red shell, but that idea was scrapped due to concerns of it looking too similar to Azure Rathalos. Eventually, the team decided on giving Seregios a golden body, as that would be new and unique. During this stage, a creature designer presented Tokuda and Fujioka with a draft that featured strange, pinecone-like scales. The two felt that this idea was just what the monster needed, and Tokuda pushed to implement it into the gameplay of the monster as well. This resulted in the creation of the Seregios' bladescale ability, as well as its rage state allowing it to change shape.[32]

Other proposed names for Seregios were:

  • Aurelos: a combination of aer (Latin for air), Regius (Latin for King) and a traditional Monster Hunter suffix "-los", going for “the king of the skies to rival Rathalos”.
  • Ceralos: a combination of celertias (Latin for high speed) and Regius.
  • Aegios: a combination of aer and Legion, which the Japanese name also utilizes.
  • Aurthelos: a combination of Aur (Latin for gold), and a traditional Monster Hunter suffix "-thalos", going for “the golden rival of Rathalos”.[33]

Trivia

  • Seregios ranked 60th place in the Top Monster competition during Monster Hunter's 20th Anniversary.[34]
  • Prior to the release of Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate, Seregios was given the nickname "Steve" by Yuri Araujo, the community manager at the time, who was asked about the name of the new Flagship Monster of the game, replying "For now, how about "Steve"?"[35]
    • When asked later why he went with "Steve" and not something closer to the real name like "Sergio", he said it was "too close to its actual name".[36]
    • Even after Seregios' name was revealed, the nickname stuck with the community, eventually even being referenced by the official Twitter account.[37]
