=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Jin Dahaad is found exclusively in the [[Iceshard Cliffs]], and is primarily active in the Frostwinds, although it can rarely be found during other weather conditions. Interaction with Jin Dahaad in the Cliffs is limited, as it will lean over a cliffside and launch attacks at hunters and other monsters, leaving if hunters damage it enough. If Jin Dahaad is repelled like this and the player is eligible for the quest, they can pursue Jin Dahaad to the [[Rimechain Peak]] to begin a quest, starting off in Area 3 instead of the starting area. Jin Dahaad can also appear at the Rimechain Peak itself, upon which players can directly start an Investigation for it from the menu.

The battle with Jin Dahaad takes place over three (four in its proper quest) areas that it moves between at specific health intervals. The first and second areas are largely nondescript, with Jin Dahaad using powerful physical attacks from its forelimbs and tail that kick up icy debris around it, as well as ice breath attacks from its mouth, with it being able to use more powerful ice breaths from the second area onwards. The second area will always have three resting [[Hirabami]] that, if awakened, will interfere with the fight by attacking Jin Dahaad for a brief time before leaving.

The four iceplates distributed across its body (Cranial, Abdominal, Tail, and Large) can be broken, with breaking them all neutralizing its forelimbs, hindlimbs, and tail's ability to release freezing air with physical attacks. The Large Iceplate will be frozen over and nearly immune to damage until all three other Iceplates are broken, with breaking it additionally disabling its use of its super attack. Attacking and breaking Jin Dahaad's legs will always knock it down and allow better access to the iceplates. Players that break one of Jin Dahaad's Iceplates will draw its aggro, indicated by a red line connecting them to the monster similar to the one caused by a [[Luring Pod (MHWilds)|Luring Pod]].

In the third area, there will be several collapsible walls that can be brought down onto Jin Dahaad, floating rubble that can be knocked down to damage Jin Dahaad and provide temporary cover from its attacks, and several crumbling structures that Jin Dahaad can climb onto before launching ice breath attacks. The crumbling structures will gradually weaken as Jin Dahaad clings to them, until they can be collapsed with the hook Slinger to send Jin Dahaad falling back down for massive damage and a knockdown. It will also periodically use a special attack where it begins sucking in air, dealing continuous chip damage to hunters, before releasing a zone-spanning ice explosion that deals immense damage. Hunters can avoid this attack by dropping the rubble from the top of the arena and using it as cover from the blast.

In its Slay quest, Jin Dahaad will move to a fourth area once its health is low enough. This area is largely the same as the third, although it lacks the crumbling walls. Jin Dahaad uses a similar moveset to its third phase, but behaves more aggressively and gains a new attack where it crystallizes their surroundings.

Unusually for a non-[[Elder Dragon]], Jin Dahaad cannot be captured. It remains vulnerable to [[Shock Trap (MHWilds)|Shock Traps]], but is immune to [[Pitfall Trap (MHWilds)|Pitfall Traps]].

After performing an ice beam attack, Jin Dahaad's head is left exposed as weak points for Focus Strikes; its chest also becomes a weak point after expelling frost from its body vents or using its nova attack. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its head or chest, Jin Dahaad will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however, there is a cool-down to this interactions that is shared between both weak points. Additionally, all of its intact iceplates, except for the Large Iceplate, become weak points whenever Jin Dahaad is knocked down.

==== Arch-Tempered ====
Arch-Tempered Jin Dahaad receives the following moveset changes:

* Combos previous attacks more aggressively; body slam may be performed twice in a row.
* Can use attacks exclusive to the final area in the third area.
* Expelling ice mist from its torso creates a short-lived whirlwind, dealing no damage but inflicting rapid Frostblight buildup.
* Downward ice explosion produces an additional delayed explosion of icicles, dealing damage and obstructing movement.
* Can perform additional new attacks.
* While coiled around structures, creates clusters of exploding icicles around players.
* Nova attack has a faster charge-up time and is a guaranteed faint if it connects. Additionally, in the final area, it produces additional clusters of exploding icicles from the ground while charging.
