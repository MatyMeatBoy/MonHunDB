=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Rey Dau is found primarily in the [[Windward Plains]] and is primarily active in the Sandtide, although it can rarely be found in other weather conditions. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]] from HR 100 onwards.

Rey Dau primarily fights by slashing and slamming its wings, which can be done from both the ground and the air. Its horns can be folded in front of its face, allowing for charged stabs and firing off bolts of lightning. As it uses physical attacks with its wings and tail, these parts will become coated in Fulgurite glass, increasing the range of their electrical attacks. These crystals can be shattered off its body with enough damage. Rey Dau's tail cannot be coated in Fulgurite if it is severed.

Eventually Rey Dau will charge up its body with electricity, causing its horns to glow brightly, and its wings and tail to immediately become coated in Fulgurite glass. This state allows Rey Dau to fire even deadlier lightning bolts, however, it can be knocked out of it by dealing enough damage to the Fulgurite crystals covering its body.

While in its nest in Area 17, Rey Dau's electrically charged attacks will electrify the Fulgurite lining the ground, creating delayed aftershocks. Additionally, the area has a pillar that can be pulled down to topple and damage Rey Dau, generating Grounding Pod ammo for the slinger in the process, allowing one to temporarily neutralize the ground's ability to conduct electricity.

Rey Dau is immune to the effects of [[Great Thunderbug|Great Thunderbugs]] and [[Shock Trap (MHWilds)|Shock Traps]] due to its adaptations to the Sandtide and its lightning.

After its charged lightning attacks, Rey Dau's folded out horns are left exposed as weak points for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its horns, Rey Dau will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however. 

==== Arch-Tempered ====
Arch-Tempered Rey Dau receives the following moveset changes:

* Previous moves are performed faster with new combos.
* Gains a new downward lightning beam that it uses to take off.
* While in a charged state, all physical attacks leave behind delayed electric aftershocks.
* Charged lightning beams create delayed electric explosions at the impact site.
* No longer exposes a weak point after its double lightning beam.
