=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Blangonga is found primarily in the [[Iceshard Cliffs]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Blangonga relies on physical blows from its powerful forearms to attack hunters. It is also capable of releasing beams of frost from its mouth that can freeze hunters in place as well as throwing snowballs.

Blangonga is typically accompanied by a pack of Blango. Blangonga is capable of issuing commands to its Blango, causing one of them to attack the hunter. While enraged, it is additionally capable of performing a special roar, causing all present Blango to rush the Hunter. Breaking the Blangonga's fangs twice prevents it from issuing any form of command.

While in its nest in Area 14, Blangonga is capable of digging into the deep snow, re-emerging below the hunter to ambush them.

While breathing frost, Blangonga's rear is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on their rear, Blangonga will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
