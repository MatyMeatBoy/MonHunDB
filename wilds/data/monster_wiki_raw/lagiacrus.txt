= Gameplay Information =
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Lagiacrus is found primarily in the [[Scarlet Forest]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Lagiacrus primarily attack by biting and whipping with its tail. It is also capable of firing off balls of electricity, as well as discharging electricity from its Shockspikes. Performing a discharge causes its Shockspikes to gradually become charged with electricity, causing them to glow increasingly bright, for some of its physical attacks to become imbued with electricity and for its discharge to grow in strength. Eventually, it will release a massive discharge, returning itself to normal. Damaging the Shockspikes enough can remove its charge, toppling it, while breaking the spikes causes it to require more discharges to build up a charge.

After reaching a certain amount of health, Lagiacrus will dive underwater in Area 19. While underwater, hunters will be limited to movement, evades and using the slinger to either pick up and fire [[Helix Piercing Pod (MHWilds)|Helix Piercing Pods]] at Lagiacrus (both from the arena and dropped by the monster), latching onto Lagiacrus and attacking with either their knife or weapon, or bringing down the rubble in the arena. Lagiacrus will use physical attacks and electric shocks to defend itself. Its mouth becomes a weak point after an electric breath attack and attacking it causes the monster to stagger.

Dealing enough damage to Lagiacrus while underwater will trigger a finisher where the hunter latches onto its head and brings it to the surface; if Lagiacrus was close to one of the spiked ruins in the underwater arena, it will additionally be thrown into the spikes for bonus damage and a guaranteed topple when it surfaces. If the Hunter does not do enough damage to Lagiacrus in time, it will create a whirlpool before unleashing a torrent of electricity that deals massive damage to hunters in an extremely large area, both underwater and on the surface.

While in its nest in Area 19, Lagiacrus is capable of diving into the water surrounding the area to ambush the hunter from below.

After a powerful electrical discharge, Lagiacrus' back is left exposed a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its back, Lagiacrus will reel backwards and be briefly knocked down.
