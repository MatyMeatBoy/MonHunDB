=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Nerscylla is found in the [[Oilwell Basin]] and [[Iceshard Cliffs]], with [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals also being found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]]. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

Nerscylla attack with its sharp claws and a myriad of different statuses. Its large retractable mandibles and back spikes are capable of poisoning, its stinger put the hunter to sleep and it can fire web shots from its abdomen that traps its target in place. Its back spikes can be broken twice to prevent them from poisoning.

Nerscylla wears the skin of a [[Gypceros]] as armour, giving it immunity to the Thunder element. This hide is, in turn, very weak to the Fire element. However, once the hide is broken, its exposed body is weak to the Thunder element and much more resistant to Fire.

While in its nest in Area 4, Nerscylla is capable of both clinging to the webbing from the ceiling, as well as digging beneath the webbing on the floor to ambush its target from beneath.

After attacking with its poisonous mandibles, they are left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its mandibles, Nerscylla will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.
