=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Gore Magala was exclusively found in the [[Iceshard Cliffs]], though [[Tempered Monsters|Tempered]] individuals could be found in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]] after Title Update 1. It can also appear in the [[Wounded Hollow]].

At first, Gore Magala primarily attacks by biting and by utilising frenzy-based projectiles, either shots along the ground or sweeping explosions, often leaving behind clouds of frenzy in its wake. It is also capable of flying. While its target is infected with its Frenzy virus, it begins to utilise attacks with its wing-arms.

During the fight, Gore Magala will gradually build up frenzy, signalled by its wings glowing purple. This process is quickened while its target is infected. Eventually, Gore Magala will perform a special roar and enter its '''Frenzy State''': extending its antennae, darkening the Area and preventing itself from leaving the area it is currently in. In this state, Gore attacks primarily using its wing-arms, as well as using frenzy-based explosions. Gore Magala is permenantly enraged while in the Frenzy State and will cause anyone fully infected with the Frenzy virus to take passive damage overtime. Dealing enough damage to it, followed by a blow to the head, will cause it to exit this state and topple it.

Due to its eyes not being fully developed, Gore Magala is immune to [[Flash Pod (MHWilds)|Flash Bombs]].

After performing a ranged frenzy explosion, Gore Magala's mouth is left exposed as a weak-point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its mouth, Gore Magala will reel back and be briefly knocked down.
