=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Guardian Rathalos is found exclusively in the [[Ruins of Wyveria]].

Much like its base counterpart, Guardian Rathalos primarily attacks with its poisonous talons and flaming breath, though it is more adept at attacking with its talons from the ground.

Most attacks performed with Guardian Rathalos's talons will cause bursts of Wylk to occur, signalled by its legs glowing white beforehand. When these attacks are performed on pools of Wylk, they will leave behind a Wylkrystal. Hitting a Wylkrystal with most forms of elemental attack will cause it to explode, dealing damage corresponding to that element. However, Guardian Rathalos' fire attacks can also cause the Wylkrystals to explode.

Like all Guardians, Guardian Rathalos is capable of regenerating any wounds inflicted upon it. However, destroying enough wounds will cause the monster to topple and prevent it from channeling Wylk into its attacks. Guardian Rathalos will re-enter its empowered state upon absorbing enough Wylk from the ground.

After performing any of its fire breaths, or a divebomb, Guardian Rathalos's mouth and legs are left exposed as weak points for Focus Strikes respectively. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its mouth or legs, Guardian Rathalos will reel back and be briefly knocked down, there is a cool-down to this interaction however that is shared between both weak points.
