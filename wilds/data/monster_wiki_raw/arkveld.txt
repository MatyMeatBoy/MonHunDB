=Gameplay Information=
''The following information is for [[Monster Hunter Wilds]]. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.''

Arkveld can be found in any locale, irrespective of the time or weather period.

Arkveld primarily attacks with its chainblades, which can extend and retract at will, by both scraping them across the ground or by whipping them at targets from a distance. It  can also use its extendable tail to stab and sweep at targets behind it.

After some time, Arkveld will enter a charged state where its chainblades split in two (for a total of four) and become charged with Dragon energy. In this state, some of its chainblade attacks become imbued with Dragon element, as well as gaining the ability to plunge its chains into the ground and release Dragon bursts. However, its chainblades will become extremely weak to elemental damage instead of immune. Dealing enough damage to a chainblade will collapse it back into its original form, while overloading both its chainblades will dispel the enhanced form, toppling it. Arkveld will exit this state when it is exhausted, but will enter it more frequently as it nears death.

Breaking one of its chainblade parts always drops a '''Crushing Chainblade Shard''', which can be picked up as a {{IconPickerOverlayAlt|MHWilds|Pod|Heavy Slicing Pod (MHWilds)|Heavy Slicing Pod|Color=White|OverlayLeft=Star}} or {{IconPickerOverlayAlt|MHWilds|Pod|Heavy Piercing Pod (MHWilds)|Heavy Piercing Pod|Color=Blue|OverlayLeft=Star}}.

In its charged state, after performing either its quadruple chain slam or its large Dragon explosion, Arkveld's chains will be left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on its chains, Arkveld will reel back and be briefly knocked down.

==== Arch-Tempered ====
Arch-Tempered Arkveld receives the following moveset changes:

* Previous attacks are performed much faster, with many having altered timings or behavior.
* Can combo numerous attacks in a row without pause, with each attack potentially chaining into several other ones.
* Can perform additional new attacks.
* If not charged, can now absorb elemental attacks that hit its chainblades to enter its charged state faster; the charged state can only be suppressed by dealing elemental attacks to the chainblades.
* Below a certain amount of HP, begins manifesting [[Guardian]] abilities to augment its charged state, giving it Wylkrystal-creating variants of many previous attacks akin to [[Guardian Arkveld]].
* While manifesting Guardian abilities, gains even more new attacks.
* After first manifesting Guardian abilities, rapidly heals any [[Wounds (MHWilds)|Wounds]] that are not destroyed quickly.
