The Talioth (タリオス, Tariosu) is a small Bird Wyvern introduced in Monster Hunter Wilds.

Summary

Menacing Bird Wyverns that typically hunt in packs using their distinctly hooked, blade-like jaws, allowing them to latch onto targets without fear of letting go. Their excellent coordination aids them in hunts, as such, it is not unlikely to witness them victoriously take on monsters much larger than themselves, overwhelming them with numbers. They prowl their arid territory, often picking off herbivores from their herd or harassing larger threats.
