The Black Eclipse Wyvern Gore Magala[c] (ゴア・マガラ, Goa Magara) is a Demi Elder introduced in Monster Hunter 4.

Summary

Black Elder Dragon-like monsters, with four legs and a pair of wingarms covered in hair-like scales that resemble a cape or cloak. As they are blind, they utilize these scales to aid their ability to sense the location and heat of other creatures.[2] Once their senses reach its peak, Gore Magala will unfurl their feelers, which are usually flush with their head, and begin walking on their wingarms as well, which are equipped with opposable hands.

Gore Magala is a major spreader of the Frenzy Virus, with the scales from their wings and breath being of capable infecting others with the virus.

Physiology

The Gore Magala is an extremely unusual creature, possessing the three sets of limbs typically seen in Elder Dragons; two pairs bear its weight while walking, while the largest third pair are powerful wingarms that end in large, developed hands. Its body is covered entirely in a thick shell of overlapping, black scales, and from the membranes of its wings grow fine hairs that fall off and regenerate continually, creating a constant stream of dander that gives Gore Magala the appearance of being clad in smoke. The scales of the Gore Magala are coated in a deep-black, cornified layer,[6][f] a hue described as so dark that it devours light rather than reflect it.[7] These properties have granted these scales various reputations, as some consider them bringers of ill-fortune,[8] whereas others view them as possessing a bewitching allure.[9] These scales overlap and reinforce each other into an armored shell that protects the monster from harm. The Gore Magala's shells in particular are noted for their resilience,[10] as they are said to harm the flesh of weaker attackers on contact.[11] Harder sections of the shelling has even been rumored to induce insanity when forged as equipment.[12]

One of the most crucial characteristics of the Gore Magala is that it is blind, possessing no visible eyes and lacking any traditional visual sense. It instead relies on an entirely unique sensory mechanism centered around the dander flowing from its wings. Fine hairs are produced constantly on the membranes of the Gore Magala's wings, which then detach to saturate the air and spread to nearby surfaces.[4] The usually folded pair of horns that adorn its crown, called Feelers,[13] have the peculiar ability to sense the presence of anything that is covered in the Gore Magala's scattered scales.[4] By spreading this black dander, the Gore Magala can therefore enhance its ability to sense its surroundings, making up for its apparent blindness.[14] The sensory acuity of the Gore Magala increases proportionally to the amount of dander that it has been able to expel.[15]

As the amount of Frenzy particles in the area increases, the underside of the Gore Magala's wings begin to glow in various colors, transitioning from blue, to purple, and then to pink. At which point the creature alights with Frenzy, extends its horns, and wails loudly, entering a condition deemed the Frenzied State.[16] This ability results in heightened ferocity and enhances the monster's abilities temporarily, although will wear off if the horns are damaged enough. It has been theorized that this behavior results from the Gore Magala temporarily giving in to the Frenzy Virus. Due to these behaviors, the Gore Magala appears in ancient legends as a demon of disease and death.[16]

The Gore Magala's dander serves a secondary purpose, as the tiny particles are the primary vectors that spread the Frenzy Virus (狂竜ウイルス, "Mad Wyvern Virus"), a mysterious condition that causes affected monsters to become violent and aggressive. It is called a virus due to its ability to spread from one victim to the next, resembling a viral disease.[4] The disease takes effect when the dander is inhaled, although open wounds can also accelerate infection.[16] Furthermore, the presence of the Frenzy Virus affects the Gore Magala's own body; accumulated clumps of the virus can crystalize into Frenzy Crystals, which the Hunter's Guild treats as highly dangerous items that must be turned in.[17] As well, some of the scales on the Gore Magala's body absorb the Frenzy Virus over time, becoming empowered enough to earn a new name: Defiled Scales.[18] One scale on the monster's body can be pushed even further beyond and imbued with unbelievable power.[19]

The well-developed, muscular wingarms of the Gore Magala are covered in highly resistant plating, which deepen in hue as the monster's hunger and madness surge.[20] While long and powerful enough to support a hexapedal stance, they are habitually folded against the Gore Magala's body, with the enormous hands resting on its shoulders. Due to their reach and power, they serve as its primary physical weapon. The claws of the wingarms are razor sharp, able to cut through living tissue with ease,[21] as well as directly infect those they slash with the Frenzy.[22] Rumors amongst artisans claim that even after these claws have been removed from the Gore Magala, they can still cause affliction to those that touch them.[23] It is a fear that, curiously, predates the official discovery of the Frenzy Virus.[16] Additionally, the Gore Magala has a flexible and whip-like tail that can be used as a devastating weapon to violently shred prey apart with its swipes.[24] Its high destructive power comes from the many reverse scales that grow along its length.[25]

The Gore Magala wings appear to be constantly writhing due to the endless stream of dander flowing from them, and it has been observed that torn off fragments from the membrane continue to move and writhe in spite of having been separated from the body.[26] Due to their role in spreading the Frenzy Virus, it is said that the mere shadow of these wings causes insanity in those they cover.[27] Sowing disease throughout the land as they flutter, these calamitous wings are unseen in the dark of the night.[28] The Gore Magala's horns typically remain hidden by being folded neatly against the head, done to protect the valuable sensory organs from damage. However, when the dander saturation in the surrounding area reaches its apex, the horns will spring outwards and reveal themselves in order to take in as much sensory data as possible.[2] When extended, the tips of the horns will glow in a foreboding purple, signalling they are wholly doused in the Frenzy.[29] Due to the high risk of infection, these horns were at one point considered forbidden spoils and ill omens by the Guild.[30]

Mysterious objects grow within the Gore Magala's body, as such, Hunters may sometimes retrieve a growth called a Nyctgem from it, which is said to imprison and swallow all nearby light.[31]

Life Cycle

The Gore Magala is the juvenile state of the Elder Dragon, Shagaru Magala. It is theorized that this bizarre species of monster either originates from, or has a special relationship with the Sanctuary near the Wyverian settlement of Cathar.[32] The Gore Magala is born when the scattered dander unique to the Shagaru Magala, which contains reproductive germ cells, overtakes a living monster. After which, should it prove to be a compatible host, the infected monster will be used as a nursery by the virus and transformed into a Gore Magala.[33][g] Due to this parasitic reproductive method, the Gore Magala is genderless.[34]

Once the Gore Magala has traveled through various places and grown to maturity,[35] it will shed its exterior shell and transition into its adult state.[36] However, should it suffer exposure to the dander of the Shagaru Magala after its birth, the special compounds in those scales suppress its maturing process. In the worst case scenario, a suppressed Gore Magala may molt incorrectly, becoming an abominable, mutated Variant, known as the Chaotic Gore Magala. Through this, it has been determined that not all Gore Magala are capable of developing past their juvenile state. As a result, the Shagaru Magala is ensured a unilateral rule where it reigns supreme over the species as a solitary ruler, permitting other Gore Magala to exist only as its vassals.[33][37]

Behavior

Although very little is known about the Gore Magala's behavior, it can often be observed twitching and convulsing as if writhing in pain, the intensity of this behavior has been suggested to be an indicator of its imminent transition into adulthood. It generally appears to roam the lands, spreading its Frenzy Virus far and wide.

Music

Main article: Gore Magala (Music)

A Cloak Concealing the Light, also known as Brilliance Eclipsed is Gore Magala's theme. It is notable for using didgeridoos as backing instruments.

Development

Monster Hunter 4

The design process for Gore Magala was centered around the desire to make the story of Monster Hunter 4 as simple and easy to grasp as possible, in order to draw in more players. This meant that the game needed a more overt villainous force the player could be pitted against. It was thus decided that the main monster of the game would need to express a sense of evil and dread.[38]

Initially, Yuya Tokuda wanted Gore Magala to walk on four legs. During the design process, one idea that emerged was to create a fight that would emphasize the new verticality that was at the center of Monster Hunter 4. This led to various concepts, including one where Gore Magala had the ability to cling to walls. Eventually the team landed on the concept of a monster that shifted from a four-legged stance to a six-legged one, which would alter its height notably and touch on that vertical design.[38]

At one point, the gameplay concept was narrowed down to "a monster that can tranform, which changes it from four-legged to six-legged". Around that same time, Yuya Tokuda was experimenting with ideas on how to make late-game gameplay more interesting. He wanted to create a system that could empower various monsters beyond just giving them more health or attack power. After some coordination, the Frenzy Virus was created as a solution to both questions - it functioned as both a way for Gore Magala to transform and a narrative device that could be used to empower much of the monster roster in an interesting and memorable way.[39]

A proposed name for Gore Magala was "Espira Magala", based on the French word for spirit.[40]

Etymology
"Gore" was chosen because it was similar to "Goa" and inferred a sense of evil and dread. The "Magala" portion was kept from the Japanese name and translates loosely to "Incarnation" in Aramaic.[40]


Trivia

  • Gore Magala ranked 5th place in the Top Monster competition during Monster Hunter's 20th Anniversary.[41]
    • Gore Magala ranked 1st place in North & South America, followed by Lagiacrus in 2nd, and Zinogre in 3rd. [42]
    • Gore Magala ranked 3rd place in Europe, preceded by Lagiacrus in 1st, and Nergigante in 2nd. [42]
  • Gore Magala was the first Large Monster in the series to have its classification changed, going from ??? to a Demi Elder in Monster Hunter Wilds.
  • Gore Magala is one of the three Flagship Monsters that were reused for multiple games, along with Rathalos and Azure Rathalos, being the Flagship for Monster Hunter 4 and Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate.
