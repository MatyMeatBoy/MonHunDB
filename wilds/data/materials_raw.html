Materials | Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki

This page was last edited on 16 April 2026, at 15:33.
From Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki


Materials are Items in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds) that can be sold or used for Upgrades or Crafting other Items, Weapons, Armor, and Equipment. Materials are typically obtained by foraging them from their environments or as drops from Monsters after carving specific Monsters for their parts. Lots of Materials can be earned by participating in hunts. In previous titles, Materials have also been sorted into different ranks and rarities, with high-ranked materials being sourced from more difficult Monsters. Find out all about what can be crafted from these Materials, its uses, the requirements, and how to obtain each required Material in Monster Hunter Wilds.
