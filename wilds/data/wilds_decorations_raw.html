Decorations

Decorations | Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki

This page was last edited on 15 June 2026, at 03:31.
From Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki


Decorations in Monster Hunter Wilds are items that can be placed into Weapons and Armor to gain or improve Skills. Equipment pieces display special icons to indicate the slot level available for decorations. Decorations are divided into different slot levels, though these levels have no impact on Hunter Rank or Skill level. They serve only to determine which types of Skills can be slotted into a given weapon or armor piece. This page covers a list of all Decorations in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds).

  • To learn more about Melding or the Melding Pot, please check our Melding Pot guide page.

How to Get Decorations in MH Wilds?

In Monster Hunter Wilds, Decorations become available after completing Chapter 2-4, Long-forgotten Flame. On this quest, you will be hunting the Nu Udra in the depths of Oilwell Basin. After this encounter, you will be introduced to a new game mechanic, "Appraisal Items". Appraisal Items contain a random Decoration that will be appraised as you accept all the Quest Rewards. Once accepted, you can either Take All or Sell All. Take all will send all item rewards to your Item Box, while Sell All will trade all your items for coins.

From this point onwards, you may get Appraisal Items for completing High Rank quests. High Rank quests are determined at the start of the hunt, based on the difficulty of the monster you are hunting. High Rank quests will have an orange star next to its rating, while a Low Rank quest will have a blue star next to its rating.

  • High Rank quests are unlocked after completing Chapter 3-5, Monster Hunter. You will be facing off against the story boss in Monster Hunter Wilds. At the end of the encounter, your Hunter Rank will be adjusted, High Rank quests will be unlocked, along with other rewards and game features.


What's New with Decorations in Monster Hunter Wilds?

Decorations are split into two categories: Weapons and Armor Decorations. In the previous Monster Hunter titles, decorations can be equipped regardless of the Equipment type, as long as the Decoration (Jewel) fits in the Decoration slot. Now, in MH Wilds, Decorations that buff your Defense, Elemental Resistances, and damage negation can only be equipped on Armor Decorations. On the other hand, any jewels or decorations that affect your Attack Power and weapon properties are suitable for Weapon Decorations only.


How Do Decoration Slots Work in MH Wilds?

Armor and weapons can have up to 3 Decoration Slots, supporting Level 1 to 3 Decorations. A Decoration's level is shown by triangles beneath its icon or a number next to its name, and must match the slot level. Lower rank gear typically supports Level 1 and 2, while Level 3 slots appear more in higher rank gear. Lower level Decorations can be placed in higher level slots, but higher-level Decorations cannot be placed in lower-level slots. Decoration Slot Level has no impact on your Hunter Rank or Skill level. They are only meant to limit the type of Skills you can use in a weapon or armor slot.

Where do I Craft Decorations in MH Wilds?

All Decorations can be crafted by Hunters in Monster Hunter Wild. This gives Hunters freedom to create their own Decorations and try to improve or round out their Builds as they see fit. The only leveling factor to know how much they will affect your build is the number of Decorations Slots and level of the Decoration slots your set has.

Decorations can be crafted at the Elder Vio in Suja, Peaks of Accord. Interact with her any time you feel like you need a new Decoration. You will need to have the desired Decoration unlocked as well as the required Materials


What is the Difference Between Weapon and Armor Decorations in MH Wilds?

In MH Wilds, Decorations are split into two categories: Weapons and Armor Decorations. Unlike previous Monster Hunter titles where decorations could be equipped regardless of equipment type, Decorations that buff your Defense, Elemental Resistances, and damage negation can only be equipped on Armor slots. Decorations that affect your Attack Power and weapon properties are suitable for Weapon slots only. Weapon Decorations are marked with a Sword icon at the upper right of the item's icon, while Armor Decorations display a Helmet marker.


What are Appraisal Items and How Do I Use Them in MH Wilds?

Appraisal Items contain a random Decoration that will be appraised as you accept all the Quest Rewards. Once accepted, you can either Take All or Sell All. Take All will send all item rewards to your Item Box, while Sell All will trade all your items for coins. Appraisal Items can be obtained as quest and bonus rewards, usually through Field Surveys and Investigations. Optional Quests may also reward them. Orbs are converted to randomly generated decorations immediately after the quest in the Appraisal Box.
