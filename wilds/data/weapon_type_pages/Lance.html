Lance

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Cut Damage

Lance (ランス ransu, "lance") is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Lance is a highly defensive weapon, that is paired with a shield and preserves mobility while guarding. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Lance Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Damage taken while in Power Guard has been further reduced.
  • Raw damage and elemental scaling for Retribution Thrust following a Perfect Guard have been increased.
  • Wide Sweep, Reverse Attack, Finishing Thrust, Finishing Twin Thrust, and Counter Rush raw damage and elemental scaling have been increased.
  • Charge Counter can now be chained into after a Perfect Guard or Guards with minimal knockback.
  • Elemental scaling for Return Thrust , Mid Thrust I, II, III, and Triple Thrust have been increased.
  • Payback Thrust, Grand Retribution Thrust, and High Thrust I, II, III raw damage have been increased.
  • The hitbox for Retribution Thrust has been enlarged.
  • Extended the distance traveled when inputting a direction with Leaping Thrust, Return Thrust, and Payback Thrust.
  • Adjusted the transition from Wide Sweep into Mid Thrust, High Thrust, and Dash Attack to be quicker.
  • The amount of weapon sharpness consumed when performing a Focus Strike: Victory Thrust was reduced.
  • After winning a Power Clash, you can now follow up with Return Thrust, Payback Thrust, or Retribution Thrust.
  • Fixed an issue where certain attacks hitting the opposite direction the player is facing during a Power Guard would not correctly transition into a Stalwart Guard.
  • Fixed an issue where guarding while moving would not properly block attacks.

Top-class defense. Low mobility but has speedy attacks with a small vulnerability window and various defensive abilities.

MHWilds Lance Patch Changes

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • You can now chain into a Charge Counter from Focus Strike: Victory Thrust.
  • Adjusted the hitbox of Focus Strike: Victory Thrust to make it easier to land.
  • Fixed an issue where certain shield attacks during Focus Strike: Resolve could unintentionally break monster wounds.

Monster Hunter Wilds Lance Changes

  • Charge Counter: You can charge up the Charge Counter as the name implies, up to three levels. This Counter Charge can be used in a way similar to offset attacks as you can release the charge with an incoming Monster attack, it will perform the double thrust counter dealing massive damage. If you fail to let go of the charge or get hit, you will fail the counter and no counterattack will occur.
  • Guarding & Perfect Guard: While holding the guard button will protect the hunter from most incoming damage, triggering a perfect guard by guarding just before the monster’s attack will reward a guard point. The difference between triggering the perfect guard with this weapon as opposed to other blocking weapons is that it will automatically attack with the shield when triggered and you can follow up with a special counter-return thrust.
  • Counters After Successful Guard: After guarding you can perform a follow-up dash attack that veteran players will be already familiar with but with perfect guard, you can trigger a more powerful charged-up Red version of the dash instantly and this dash can be ended with a powerful finisher for more damage.

MHWilds Lance Weapon Traits

  • Its thrusting attacks are swift and safe to execute.
  • Take advantage of its variety of guard-related moves and counters.
  • Thrust-based damage weapon.
  • Charge attack hit multiple times.
  • Exceptionally high range and counter-attacks.
  • Strongest blocking ability in the game, matched only by its cousin, the Gunlance.
  • Top-class defense. Low mobility but has speedy attacks with a small vulnerability window and various defensive abilities.
  • Guarding: Yes (with Counterattack)
Lance Tips
  • Triple Thrust and Counter Thrust: You can perform a fourth-level attack after Mid Thrust III or High Thrust III

MHWilds Lance Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • The most defensive-minded Weapon in the game, thanks to is defensive and guarding capabilities.
  • Pin-point attacks make it easy to target specific monster parts.
  • One of the most forgiving when it comes to the counterattack style of play.
  • Top-class defense. Low mobility but has speedy attacks with a small vulnerability window and various defensive abilities.

Weapon Damage Types

  • May feel like the least mobile among all weapons until you master its hop and poke mechanics.
  • Trades damage for supreme defense. Hunts tend to take longer with it, until you can build up consistency with attacks.
  • Generally tougher to master and may not feel as flashy or exciting as other Weapons.

Monster Hunter Wilds Lance Controls on Consoles

  • Mid Thrust I: Performs a basic combo of thrusts in front of you. Using this While in Focus Mode Will allow you to move in the direction inputted with + during the combo. You can string up to three thrusts, with Mid Thrust III, chaining into Triple Thrust with
  • High Thrust I: A basic combo starter that thrusts upwards. You will thrust in the direction you are aiming while in Focus Mode The attack can be repeated up to three times. After performing High Thrust III, Counter-thrust can be used - its damage increasing the longer is held. Timing this attack with a monster's attack will allow you to perform the powerful Double Counter-thrust.
  • +Leaping Thrust: A thrusting attack that steps forward.
  • +During Combo - Wide Sweep: A large sweeping attack that can be performed during combos.
  • Guard: Use your shield to Guard, which can also be performed during combos. You will automatically counterattack after guarding if the guard reaction is small enough. Using Perfect Guard will also allow you to perform a powerful counterattack. Press + to move forward while holding your shield up . Pressing after this will perform a Shield Attack, while pressing will perform Leaping Thrust.
    • ++Guard Dash
      • Shield Attack
        • Leaping Thrust
  • +Power Guard: Hunker down and put up a nearly impenetrable Guard. While your stamina will drain in this state, your reaction from guarding will be greatly reduced, allowing you to easily counterattack. Your counterattack's damage will be boosted with every hit you Guard against.
  • ++Dash Attack: Charge forward with your lance in hand. You can attack multiple times while running, but your stamina will slowly drain. Both your speed and attack power will rise after running for a short time. You can step and turn while preserving your momentum during your Dash Attack using + and respectively. You can perform a powerful thrust attack using your momentum with or. An even more powerful Finishing Twin Thrust is also possible when above a certain speed.
    • + Dash Step / Dash Turn
    • orFinishing Thrust
  • After Successful Guard - Return Thrust: A quick counterattack that can be performed after a Guard. It is easy to chain into a combo, and its speed allows you to Guard again shortly after an attack.
  • After Successful Guard - Payback Thrust: A forward-stepping counterattack that can be performed after using Guard. While 1 it deals more damage than the light version, it will leave you open for longer.
  • Focus Strike: Victory Thrust: A shield attack that is effective against wounds. Hitting a monster's wound or weak point will allow you to deal damage to multiple places. Press during a thrust attack to end the attack at any time.
