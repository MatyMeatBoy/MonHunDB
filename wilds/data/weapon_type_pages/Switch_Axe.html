Switch Axe

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Cut Damage

Switch Axe (スラッシュアックス surasshu akkusu, "slash axe") is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Switch Axe is a massive and bulky type of weapon, that performs devastating attacks. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Switch Axe Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Relaxed the offset detection for Axe: Offset Rising Slash, making it easier to trigger an offset attack.
  • The offset buildup for Axe: Offset Rising Slash has been increased.

Lets you morph between two modes. Axe Mode has better reach, while Sword Mode features faster attacks.

MHWilds Switch Axe Patch Changes

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • It is now easier to adjust your distance to monsters when using Unbridled Slash and Full Release Slash while in Focus Mode.
  • The backward movement triggered by Element Discharge Finisher can now be reduced slightly by tilting the left stick in the player character's direction.
  • Significantly increased the elemental scaling of follow-up explosions from sword attacks when using Element Phials.
  • Significantly increased the elemental scaling of follow-up explosions from Unbridled Slash, Full Release Slash, and Axe: Follow-up Morph Slash when using Element Phials.
  • Axe: Follow-up Heavy Slam and Axe: Follow-up Morph Slash raw damage has been increased.
  • The amount of Switch Gauge recovered when performing basic attacks in Axe Mode has been increased.
  • Increased the raw damage and Amp Gauge recovery of Axe: Morph Rising Double Slash, and reduced its Switch Gauge consumption.
  • Sword: Triple Slash raw damage and amp gauge recovery amount have been increased.
  • The amount of Switch Gauge consumed when performing Sword: Overhead Slash has been decreased.
  • Fixed an issue where the damage scaling from the Rapid Morph skill was not being applied to Jumping Morph Slash when performed upon landing.

Monster Hunter Wilds Switch Axe Changes

  • Already a versatile weapon, the Switch Axe has received numerous new tools and attacks that boost the weapons’ responsiveness and with the addition of the new offset attacks and counterattacks to both of its forms, the Switch Axe is in the most responsive state it has ever been in the series.
  • Knockback is now negated when using the Full Release Slash move.
  • Full-Release Slash: The more flashy additions to the Switch Axe are the Full-Release Slash and the Unbridled Slash. Although these moves naturally flowed into the classic element discharge combo finisher, these new moves are not exclusive to combo finishers. As you play with the Switch Axe, you will find that these moves can be performed at any time in Sword Mode, which is another reason we mentioned them in this Monster Hunter Wilds Switch Axe Guide. When you perform these moves, you spin around and deal a huge amount of elemental damage but the trade-off is that these moves consume a large amount of the Switch Gauge and end with you morphing back into the Axe Form at the end of the attack. You can perform the more powerful Release Slash in the Amped State and when your Amp Gauge is full dealing even more damage.
  • Offset Rising Slash: The Switch Axe also gains access to the new powerful offset attack with the Offset Rising Slash, which if you can connect it with the monster’s attack, you’ll topple the monster and then you can follow up with one of the two follow-up attacks, the Heavy Slam, which puts your axe into the Power Axe Mode or the Follow-up Morph Slash, which will allow you to directly morph into the sword for sword attacks. The Power Axe Mode also can be accessed by doing the Wild Swing Attack and ending the combo with a heavy slam.
  • Sword Mode: The Sword Mode gains access to a similarly powerful attack named the Counter-Rising Slash. The Counter-Rising Slash can be performed at any moment or after any attack. This move makes the hunter ready for a second and when the monster’s attack hits you during this time, you’ll counter with two attacks in a row from which you can directly flow into the regular combos or the Heavenward Flurry after Double Slash for powerful attacks.
  • Zero Sum Discharge: After latching on make a return from the previous games and as before you need to be in the Amped State to perform this attack. With the Focus Mode, you can aim at the part you want to latch onto and after landing it unleashes a multi-hit discharge and explosion like the elemental discharge.
  • Focus Assault: Morph Combination: The focus mode strike, named, Focus Assault: Morph Combination. Focus mode has two different finishers depending on the mode in which you end your assault on the Wound. When you connect the strike to a Wound, the strike starts with wild swings and during this, you can freely morph between the axe and the sword states if you end it in the axe mode, it will end with a heavy slam leading into the power axe state. If you end the assault in the Sword Mode, it will end with an element discharge finisher which deals more damage but consumes some of your Switch Gauge.
  • Another thing to note during mounting attacks, your attacks will refill your Switch Gauge slightly so you can use this to top it up during hunts, which is a great tip for this Monster Hunter Wilds Switch Axe Guide.

MHWilds Switch Axe Weapon Traits

  • A versatile weapon that can switch between two different modes.
  • Axe mode has good reach and mobility.
  • A complex weapon with two forms and a phial system.
  • Sword mode has extra damage, uses the phial's bonus, and lets you unleash faster attacks.
  • The switch axe morphs between axe and sword modes. Axe mode boasts power and long reach, while sword mode features faster attacks and explosive finishers.
  • Lets you morph between two modes. Axe Mode has better reach, while Sword Mode features faster attacks.
  • Guarding: None
Switch Axe Tips
  • Switch Gauge (Axe Mode): Attacking in Axe Mode fills up the Switch Gauge.
  • Switch Gauge (Sword Mode): Attacking in Sword Mode consumes the Switch Gauge. Using it up switches you back to Axe Mode.
  • Charge up the Switch Gauge: Attack in Axe Mode to fill the Switch Gauge quickly.
  • Offset Attack: You can offset a monster's attack by parrying it at just the right moment with an Offset attack.
Switch Axe Weapon Properties
  • Sword Mode ↔ Axe Mode: Attacking in Axe Mode will fill the Switch Gauge, while attacking in Sword Mode will use it up. Switch between modes while fighting to ensure success.
  • Switch Gauge: A gauge that fills as you land attacks in Axe Mode. You can morph into Sword Mode when the Switch Gauge is at 30%. The gauge will fill over time when your weapon is sheathed.
  • Power Axe Mode: Increases damage while in Axe Mode. Also increases the rate the Switch Gauge fills while attacking.
  • Amp Gauge: A gauge that fills as you land attacks in Sword Mode. You can enter the Amped State when the Amp Gauge has filled completely.
  • Amped State: Small explosions dealing damage based on your current phial type occur automatically when attacking in Sword Mode.
  • Phial Type: Attacking in Sword Mode will consume the Switch Gauge, adding an effect based on the phial type. Some phials will fill the gauge easier than others.
    • Power Phial: Boosts physical damage.
    • Element Phial: Boosts elemental damage.
    • Poison Phial: Adds Poison.
    • Paralysis Phial: Adds Paralysis.
    • Dragon Phial: Adds dragon element.
    • Exhaust Phial: Adds Stun/Energy Drain.

MHWilds Switch Axe Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • A versatile weapon that can switch between a sword or axe mode.
  • Phials are used to boost the performance and capability of the weapon.
  • The Switch Axe is mainly a DPS weapon that favors an offensive style of play.
  • Very effective at dishing out elemental and status damage.
  • Let's you morph between two modes: Axe mode has better reach, while Sword mode features faster attacks.

Weapon Disadvantages

  • Does not have any defensive moves or attacks, not recommended for hunters that are inexperienced in dodging attacks.
  • Some attacks cause the wielder to become stationary, allowing yourself to be open to a monster's attacks.
  • Positioning is key with Switch Axes. Both in avoiding being caught, as well as in dealing damage.

Monster Hunter Wilds Switch Axe Controls on Consoles

Controls (Axe)

  • Axe: Overhead Slash . Press after Axe: Overhead Slash to perform Axe: Side Slash, then for Axe: Spiral Burst Slash. Axe: Spiral Burst Slash will replenish a large amount of your Switch Gauge. You can also use + for the mid-range, forward-moving attack Axe: Forward Slash.
  • Axe: Wild Swing . will allow you to continuously use Axe: Wild Swing. After using Axe: Wild Swing at least twice, press for Axe: Heavy Slam, which will transition you into Power Axe Mode. Press + to use Axe: Forward Overhead Slash, a long range, forward-moving attack.
    • Axe: Heavy Slam
  • Axe: Offset Rising Slash +. An upwards slash that can Offset monster attacks. If you land a Perfect Offset, press , for Axe: Follow-up Heavy Slam, transitioning you into Power Axe Mode, or for Axe: Follow-up Morph Slash, which will transition you into Sword Mode.
    • Axe: Follow-up Heavy Slam (' Upon Perfect Offset)'
    • Axe: Follow-up Morph Slash ( Upon Perfect Offset)
  • Axe: Fade Slash (+ During Combo) A high-reaching Attack while moving backwards.
  • Morph . You can Morph into Sword Mode when the Switch Gauge is at 30% or higher. You can also use Axe: Morph Slash with+. If you have insufficient Switch Gauge, you will perform Reload instead.
  • Focus Assault: Morph Combination +. A slashing attack that is effective against wounds. Hitting a monster's wound or a weak point will land multiple hits that chain into a Power Axe Finisher, dealing massive damage. Alternatively, use mid-attack to morph into Sword Mode to conclude with an Element Discharge Finisher.
    • Element Discharge Finisher

Controls (Sword)

  • Sword: Overhead Slash . Press to perform Sword: Right Rising Slash after Sword: Overhead Slash. Following Sword: Right Rising Slash, you can press to perform Sword: Left Rising Slash.
  • Sword: Double Slash . Press after Sword: Double Slash for Sword: Heavenward Flurry, which will fill the Amp Gauge by a large amount.
  • Sword: Counter Rising Slash +. Perform a Sword: Counter Rising Slash right when a monster attacks and you will unleash a two-hit counterattack. Succeeding will also prevent you from being knocked back or sent flying. Press after the counterattack to follow up with a Sword: Heavenward Flurry.
  • Element Discharge +: Press repeatedly after Element Discharge for an explosive finishing attack. You will automatically morph into Axe Mode after the Explosion.
  • Zero Sum Discharge +: You can unleash a Zero Sum Discharge by performing an Element Discharge in the Ampwed State. Once grabbed onto the side of the monster, use to perform a Zero Sum Discharge Finisher. After the finisher, your weapon will return to Axe Mode.
  • Unbridled Slash ++: A powerful attack available in Sword Mode. An extra damage-dealing explosion will occur if used in the Amped State. You will morph into Axe Mode after the attack.
  • Full Release Slash ++: A powerful attack only usable in Amped State. You can also combo with into this with after an Element Discharge. This attack will return you to Axe Mode.
  • Morph . Morph into Axe Mode. You can also perform Sword: Morph Slash with +.
  • Focus Assault: Morph Combination +: A slashing attack that is effective against wounds. Hitting a monster's wound or a weak point will land multiple hits that chain into an Element Discharge Finisher, dealing massive damage. Alternatively, use mid-attack to morph into Sword Mode to conclude with a Power Axe Finisher.
    • Power Axe Finisher
