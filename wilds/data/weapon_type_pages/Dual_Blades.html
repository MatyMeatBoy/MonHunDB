Dual Blades

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Cut Damage

Dual Blades (双剣 sou ken, "paired swords") are one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). Dual Blades are wielded in pairs and focus more on fast attacks rather than raw damage. Like all Weapons, they have their own unique set of moves, along with an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials you use.


Designed for continous, ferocious attacks. Demon Mode makes your hits more powerful but will gradually drain stamina.

Patch Notes of Dual Blades in MH Wilds

NOTE: MHWilds Dual Blades Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Adjusted Focus Strike: Turning Tide so that it can now be chained into Turning Tidedrill, a new finisher action that delivers a multi-hit attack to a targeted wound, by pressing △ (PS5) / Y (Xbox) / default Right Click (PC).

  • Movement speed during Demon Mode has been increased.
  • The window for a successful Perfect Evade has been extended very slightly.

Patch Changes of Dual Blades in MH Wilds

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • You can now change your movement direction mid-movement when entering Demon Mode right after a dodge.
  • While in Demon Boost Mode (during Demon Mode), chaining a dodge into a Blade Dance now chains into a Blade Dance II instead. (However, this will default to a Blade Dance I if there is not enough Demon Gauge left.)
  • Blade Dance II responsiveness has been improved.
  • Movement speed during Demon Mode has been increased.
  • Basic moves like Sixfold Demon Slash, Demon Flurry Rush, and Demon Flurry have had their raw damage increased.
  • Screw Slicer raw damage, elemental scaling, and ailment scaling have been greatly increased.
  • Focus Strike: Turning Tide raw damage has been increased.
  • Heavenly Blade Dance raw damage has been increased.
  • Blade Dance elemental scaling and ailment scaling have been slightly decreased, and raw damage has been increased.
  • The raw damage boost from Demon Boost Mode has been slightly decreased, and the elemental scaling boost has been increased.
  • Fixed an issue where the Power Prolonger skill was making the duration of Demon Boost Mode buffs last longer than intended.
  • Fixed an issue where the camera would shake unintentionally during Demon Mode.

Changes of Dual Blades in MH Wilds

  • Blade Dance: When in the Demon Mode or Archdemon Mode, you can perform Blade Dance which performs a flurry of slashes. In previous games, this attack locks you in place for the duration of the combo and leaves you wide open to monster attacks. In Monster Hunter Wilds, Blade Dance has been broken up into three different attacks where you can stop your combo or reposition as needed between each strike.
  • Demon Flurry: When you go into Archdemon mode, the Blade Dance is replaced by a weaker version called Demon Flurry which is also split into two attacks much like the Blade Dance. By utilizing the Focus Mode, you can reposition yourself while facing the monster, letting you hop backward or to the side providing you with the escape options you need, especially if you are out of stamina as using the Demon Flurry only uses the demon gauge instead of the stamina bar.
  • Perfect Evade: Another cool addition to the Dual Blades is the perfect evade mechanic. By evading a monster’s attack at the exact time just before it strikes, you will trigger a perfect evade which will negate the incoming damage and provide a buff known as the Demon Boost mode which lasts for twelve seconds causing the hunter to glow blue. This buff increases attack damage and also turns all of your dodges into damaging spin attacks. This newly added mechanic rewards players who excel at dodge timing.
  • Focus Strike': Turning Tide': The Focus Strike gives the Dual Blade users a very familiar looking attack much like the Demon Flight. The Focus Strike: Turning Tide while in focus mode, has the hunter bring down both of their blades before launching into the air and if the strike connects to a wound, the hunter will hook onto the monster and do a spinning blade dance which runs through the entire length of the monster. You can maximize the damage output using this attack by inflicting the most amount of wounds on the monster and then bursting them all simultaneously with this attack dealing copious amounts of damage.

Weapon Traits of Dual Blades in MH Wilds

  • Their rapid nature makes Dual Blades ideal for applying status effects, as they hit incredibly fast.
  • Fast and agile, the Dual Blades are perfect for chain-long and high-damage combos.
  • Entering Demon Mode will boost the damage of your attacks.
  • Archdemon Mode boosts the damage of certain attacks even further.
  • Designed for continuous, ferocious attacks.
  • Demon Mode makes your hits more powerful, but will gradually drain Stamina.
  • Guarding: None

Dual Blades Properties

  • Demon Mode: Boosts attacks, movement speed, and evasion, and makes you immune to knockback. Your stamina will gradually deplete while Demon Mode is active. Demon Mode ends if manually canceled with Exit Demon Mode or when your stamina reaches zero.
  • Demon Gauge: Landing attacks while in Demon Mode fills the Demon Gauge. Once the gauge is full, you will enter Archdemon Mode instead of exiting Demon Mode. The Demon Gauge will gradually decrease over time, or be consumed by performing certain attacks.
  • Archdemon Mode: A state in which a number of your attacks are empowered. These boosted attacks will consume the Demon Gauge when used, and Archdemon Mode will end when the gauge fully empties.
  • Demon Boost Mode: An empowered state entered after a successful Perfect Evade. lt boosts I both regular and element damage, and allows you to attack while dodging.

Monster Hunter Wilds Dual Blades Controls on Consoles

  • Double Slash: A basic combo starter. Press to perform Double Slash Return Stroke, then press again for Circle Slash.
  • Lunging Strike: A slashing attack that advances in the direction of Pressing again will perform a Spinning Slash.
  • Demon Mode: Your attack, movement speed, and evasion will all be boosted during Demon Mode. You will also be immune to knockbacks.
    • +Blade Dance I: Blade Dance is a powerful series of attacks that consumes the Demon Gauge.
      • +Blade Dance II: Blade Dance is a powerful series of attacks that consumes the Demon Gauge.
        • +Blade Dance III: Blade Dance is a powerful series of attacks that consumes the Demon Gauge.
  • +Demon Flurry I: A series of attacks that can only be performed in Archdemon Mode and consumes the Demon Gauge. Useto maneuver the attack. Chain Demon Flurry and Blade Dance together with
    • +Demon Flurry II
  • Demon Dodge (During Demon or Archdemon Mode): A quick dodge that can evade attacks. Successfully performing a Perfect Evade will allow you to attack while dodging, and will buff you for a short time.
  • +Focus Strike: Turning Tide: A slashing attack effective against wounds. Hitting a monster's wound will allow you to perform Midair Spinning Blade Dance, an attack that spans the length of the monster and can destroy multiple wounds.
