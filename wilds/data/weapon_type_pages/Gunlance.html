Gunlance

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Cut Damage, Special

Gunlance (ガンランス ganransu, "Gunlance") is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Gunlance is a heavy lance that possess a cannon affixed to it, allowing hunters to use both of them in one weapon to deal damage. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Gunlance Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Fixed an issue where, under certain conditions, performing Burst Fire while the skill Load Shells Lv 2 was active would result in fewer hits than intended.

Combines long-reaching thrusts with shell fire. Has low mobility but great guarding capabilities and supports a variety of hard-hitting ammo such as the Wyvern's Fire

MHWilds Gunlance Patch Changes

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • Fixed an issue where, when a critical hit occurs during multiple hits of certain actions, such as Multi Wyrmstake Full Blast, there was an unintended high chance that subsequent hits would also be critical hits.

Monster Hunter Wilds Gunlance Changes

  • Shelling Attacks: With the change in Monster Hunter Wilds removing the sharpness drain from shelling with the Gunlance, you can have more uptime dealing damage to monsters before needing to Sharpen your Gunlance. Another welcome change in Wilds is that you can now move between each Shell, making it easier to reposition and combo into other moves. You can also hold down the Shelling button to charge up your shelling until the number of Shells you want to use has been loaded up.
  • Guarding: With the addition of the Perfect Guard in Monster Hunter Wilds, the Gunlance’s defensive capabilities received some improvements. The Gunlance now has the added ability to Perfect Guard while reloading. What this means is, that it makes it easier to flow into a combo after reloading when you do a Perfect Guard, such as going into an Overhead Slam. Perfect Guarding and guarding will trigger a Power Clash more often and winning a Clash will shoot all of your shells dealing massive damage. You can then follow up with a Wyrmstake Full Blast for even more damage if you combo into it a Moving Wide Sweep after the shelling.
  • Moving Wide Sweep: The Moving Wide Sweep is a new tool made available to the Gunlance in Wilds and it has various powerful follow-up options such as the Wyrmstake Fullblast or Wyvern Fire Blast. These can be easily achieved with either a quick shelling or a quick overhead smash into the sweep followed by the Wyrmstake or Wyvern fire.
  • Multi-Wyrmstake Full Blast: The Multi-Wyrmstake Full Blast has some hidden mechanics that are easily missed. The blast ends with a guard point, meaning you can continue to be aggressive and press the attack. Even though it expends all your shells, you can quickly reload during the move to reload all your shells.
  • Wyvern's Fire Blast: You are now able to store two of Wyvern’s Fire Blasts and the charges can be recovered by attacking. Shelling also recovers a greater amount of the Wyvern’s Fire Charge and can be used for some quick recovery. In Wilds, the attack also has a longer reach as well as having two different types that can be performed. The first is the standard charge and the second one being a quick variation that can be performed as a follow-up stun action after some actions with the most common being, the Perfect Guard or the Full Burst.
  • Focus Strike: With the addition of focus mode in Monster Hunter Wilds, the Gunlance’s focus strike, called Drake Auger, is a thrusting attack that turns your gunlance into a drill, doing continuous damage and ending with a Wyrmstake Cannon. The hunter can also end Drake Auger early and go into the Wyrmstake Cannon faster.
  • Shell Types: Different Shelling types make a return for the Gunlance in Monster Hunter Wilds and are similar to previous games in the series. Different Gunlances will have different shelling types and power. There are three different shelling types: Normal, Wide, and Long, with the wide type having a short range but having a greater area of effect like a shotgun, and the Long type having a slightly longer range compared to the Normal shelling type. Shells also come in three different power variations: Normal, Slightly Weak and Slightly Strong which determines how much damage they do.

MHWilds Gunlance Weapon Traits

  • Damage based on thrust attacks, firing blanks and explosive shells,
  • Features a heavy shield to guard against incoming attacks. Strongest blocking ability in the game, matched only by its cousin, the Lance.
  • High range and counter-attacks.
  • You can make use of sidesteps to make small positioning adjustments.
  • Utilize a diverse range of shell-based attacks.
  • Combines long-reaching thrusts with shell fire. Has low mobility but great guarding capabilities and supports a variety of hard-hitting ammo.
  • Guarding: Yes

Gunlance Tips

  • Reload: Press + to reload. You cannot fire shells when you are out of ammo.

Gunlance Properties

  • Shelling Type: There are three types of shelling: Normal, Wide, and Long. Normal shells have the most capacity. Wide shells make for more powerful Shelling and Wyvern's Fire attacks. Long shells make for more powerful Wyrmstake Cannon attacks.
  • Ammo: Ammo required to use Shelling. A shell is consumed with each firing. They can be recovered by using Reload or Quick Reload.
  • Wyrmstake Shot: Ammo required to use Wyrmstake Cannon. Consumed each time Wyrmstake Cannon is fired and can be recovered with Reload.
  • Wyvern Gauge: A gauge required to use Wyvern's Fire - an attack that inflicts powerful damage that includes fire damage, cutting through flesh.A gauge required to use Wyvern's Fire - an attack that inflicts powerful damage that includes fire damage, cutting through flesh. Up to two segments can be charged at once. Using Wyvern's Fire consumes one segment.

Monster Hunter Wilds Gunlance Controls on Consoles

  • Lateral Thrust I: A forward thrust attack. Useto chain into Lateral Thrust ll, then again to use Wide Sweep.
  • +Lunging Upthrust: A forward-stepping thrust. It can be chained into Overhead Smash with + , then Burst Fire with or Wide Sweep
    • +Overhead Smash
      • Burst Fire
      • Wide Sweep
  • After Wide Sweep - Wyrmstake Cannon: An attack that fires your Wyrmstake Cannon. It deals repeated damage to the part of monster it hits before exploding, dealing fire damage that cuts through the monster‘s hide.
  • +Wyrmstake Full Blast: An attack that fires all remaining shells and the Wyrmstake Cannon. Press or after Wyrmstake Full Blast to use Multi Wyrmstake Full Blast.
  • Shelling: Attack that fires ammo. Deals Fire damage that cuts through the thickness of the monster's hide. You can perform Shelling while moving in the direction of your choosing usingand +after attacking. You can perform Shelling while moving in the direction of your choosing using and + after attacking.
    • + Moving Wide Sweep
  • Charged Shelling: An attack that charges shells before firing them all at once, dealing fire damage that cuts through the monster's hide.
  • +Rising Slash: An upwards slash using the gunlance. Pressafter the attack to fire a shell, orto perform Overhead Smash.
  • Guard: Presswhile guarding to perform Guard Thrust l. Upon successfully executing a Perfect Guard, press for Overhead Smash.
  • +Reload: Reloads shells and the Wyrmstake Cannon. You will automatically block attacks immediately after beginning the reload.
  • +During Combo - Quick Reload: Reloads shells only. You will automatically block attacks immediately after beginning the reload.
  • ++Wyvern's Fire: Use one segment of the Wyvern Gauge to unleash the powerful Wyvern's Fire, dealing fire damage that cuts through the monster's hide.
  • +Focus Strike: Drake Auger: A thrusting drill attack that is effective against wounds. Hitting a monster's wound or weak point will extend the length of the attack and allow you to finish by firing the Wyrmstake Cannon. You cna also fire the Wyrmstake Cannon early by pressing during the drill attack.
