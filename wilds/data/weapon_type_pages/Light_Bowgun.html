Light Bowgun

Weapon Type Ranged
Damage Type Shot (Ammo) Damage

Light Bowgun (ライトボウガン raito boogan, "light bowgun") is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Light Bowgun is a multipurpose-ranged weapon, that has a heavier variant called Heavy Bowgun. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Light Bowgun Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Adhesive Ammo and Wyvernblast raw damage have been increased.
  • Increased the recovery speed of the Rapid Fire Gauge when using Exhaust Ammo, Poison Ammo, Paralysis Ammo, or Sleep Ammo.
  • The reload speed for Adhesive Ammo and Focus Blast: Eagle Strike has been increased.
  • The raw damage and elemental scaling for Flaming Ammo, Water Ammo, Thunder Ammo, Freeze Ammo, and Dragon Ammo have been slightly increased.
  • The amount of Rapid Fire Gauge consumed while using Dragon Ammo in Rapid Fire Mode has been decreased.
  • The amount of Rapid Fire Gauge recovered while using Pierce Ammo has been increased.
  • Fixed an issue where only one projectile out of the multiple projectiles fired with the light bowgun's Spread Ammo would receive the scaling from the Ballistics skill.

A higly mobile weapon with mid-range firing attacks. Good for quick fire and evasion. Rapid Fire lets you expend energy to fire multiple rounds of ammo.

Monster Hunter Wilds Light Bowgun Changes

  • Light Bowgun Mods & Special Ammo: In Monster Hunter Wilds, there are now two customizable categories for each Bowgun, Mods and Special Ammo slots. The Light Bowgun will have 2 slots for mods and a single slot for Special ammo.
  • The mod slot will allow you to choose which ammo type you are using and will have more shots per magazine. Another thing to note is that the two mod slots will offer different Magazines and mods. Generally, the upper one is exclusive to magazines, and the lower one has access to Upgrades.
  • For the special ammo for the Light Bowgun, you will be able to choose between the Wyvernblast Mine and the Adhesive Ammo. The Wyvernblast special ammo will put down a mine on the ground that will explode after a short time and function similar to the Wyvernblast in the previous games. The Adhesive ammo option will allow you to fire a shot that will stick to a monster for a certain amount of time before blowing up. We will go into how these work in detail later.
  • Rapid Fire Mode: By going into Rapid-Fire Mode, you attach an ammo drum to your Bowgun, and allow you to use the brand new Gauge system. The Rapid-Fire Gauge increases the rate of fire of your shots. This only works with specific ammo types and what ammo can be used with the rapid-fire mode will differ depending on the Light Bowgun you are using.
  • Burst Step: The Burst Step allows you to shoot while dodging and can be performed by pressing the evade button after you fire a shot. The Burst Step dodge also seems to vary from ammo to ammo ranging from a quick hop to a slower dive. Doing a dodge step while out of ammo will lead to a long-distance dodge move called the Side-Step Reload.
  • Chaser Shots: Chaser shots are follow-up shots that you can fire after firing a normal shot and they are more powerful than normal shots. Chaser Shots take away your mobility while you are firing, so they are best used when you have a good opening. It can also be used in Rapid-Fire mode and the shots can be chained into the Rapid Chaser Shots. It is a quick-fire attack that ends with a particularly powerful shot. After either the Chaser Shot or the Rapid Chaser Shot, you can change into either a Burst Step or a Sidestep Reload if you don’t have ammo loaded.
  • Ammo: Even though there are no new ammo types added to the game, some of the ammo now functions differently when compared to the previous games in the franchise. The normal ammo is now the rapid-fire variant from the previous iterations and both the Spread and Piercing shots now have unlimited ammo. These changes mean that you will be spending fewer resources to craft these ammo types.
  • Special Ammo: The Wyvernblast special ammo puts down a mine at your feet. This mine will explode by itself after a certain amount of time or if your shots damage or pass near the mine, the mine will send up an explosion up to three times before disappearing completely. This ammo is a great way to damage monsters that are toppled or stunned as it requires the monster to be near the mine for maximum damage.
  • Focus Blast: Eagle Strike - The attack will shoot out a grenade that explodes on impact destroying all wounds in the explosion’s radius. Holding down the attack button will let you charge up the shot dealing even more damage.
  • Even though there are no special riding attacks with the Seikret for the Light Bowgun, it is special in that you can use all the regular ammo types while you are mounted and attack the monster for even more mobility although you cannot use the rapid-fire mode, or the special ammo while you are mounted. The Seikret also offers an excellent option for mounting with the ability to perform a jumping reload.

MHWilds Light Bowgun Weapon Traits

  • A mid-range weapon that can fire different types of ammunition.
  • In MH: Wilds, you no longer need to expend resources for base damage sources (normal, pierce, and spread ammo). This means you don't need to worry about replenishing your supplies when you load into different areas (As long as the gauge is not empty). However, for elemental ammo, you still need to expend your resources, to encourage players to plan ahead and manage their supplies in hunting.
  • High mobility. Great at quick attacks and dodges.
  • Use Rapid Fire energy to fire in quick succession.
  • Energy regenerates over time when you successfully land hits.
  • Fast fire rate and short to moderate range.
  • Variety of ammo types with special effects.
  • The Bowgun can be used to attack monsters from long range. There are two types of Bowgun: the Heavy Bowgun, which is generally slower, more powerful, and has access to high-damage ammo, and the Light Bowgun, which allows for greater mobility and evasive capabilities, and faster shooting.
  • A highly mobile weapon with mid-range firing attacks. Good for quick fire and evasion. Rapid Fire lets you expend energy to fire multiple rounds of ammo.
  • Guarding: None.

Light Bowgun Tips

  • Chaser Shot: Press after firing to execute a Chaser Shot, a powerful attack that hits harder than normal.
  • Rapid Fire Mode: Rapid Fire consumes energy to let you fire many shots in quick succession.
  • Fire Normal Ammo: Normal ammo is effective against monsters at close or medium-range.
  • Fire Pierce Ammo: Pierce ammo is effective against monsters at a great distance.
  • Fire Spread Ammo: Spread Ammo is effective against monsters at close or close-range.
  • Wait for Ammo to Auto-Recover: To fire Focus Blast: Eagle Strike, wait for ammo to recover automatically.

Light Bowgun Weapon Properties

  • Rapid Fire Mode: Fire Multiple Shots of certain types of ammo by consuming the Rapid Fire Gauge. The gauge will automatically replenish in Standard Mode, but can also be recovered by landing hits on Monsters. The amount restored depends on the type of ammo used.
  • Special Ammo: Wyvernblast — Places a bomb that detonates when attacked. This explosive ammo will gradually be restored over time.

MHWilds Light Bowgun Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • Highly versatile ranged weapon with a wide selection of ammunition to match certain playstyles and monster weaknesses.
  • Has support capabilities in parties.
  • High mobility compared to its heavy counterpart, the Heavy Bowgun.

Weapon Damage Types

  • Heavy on resource management.
  • Trades damage output and defensive capability for mobility.
  • Builds require a high level of specialization in order to achieve good damage output and combat efficiency.

Monster Hunter Wilds Light Bowgun Controls on Consoles

Standard Mode

  • Fire : Fires loaded ammo straight ahead. Use Aim to fire in a chosen direction.
  • Aim/Focus : Use to ready your bowgun and easily aim at your targets. Ammo is fired straight ahead by default, but can be fired in specific directions by aiming
    Press + for an attack that's particularly effective on wounds.
    Press while firing to increase potency and explosive range.
    • Focus Blast: Eagle Strike Shot Shot +
  • Chaser Shot (After Firing): Press after firing for a Power Shot, a stronger attack that also restores more energy. It cannot be chained after Focus Blast or using Special Ammo.
  • Reload : Reload the selected ammo. Use to reload ammo in order.
  • Switch Modes: Switch your current firing mode to Rapid Fire Mode, allowing you to rapidly fire certain types of ammo. Pressing again will switch you back to Standard Mode.
  • Special Ammo+: Allows you to use the Special Ammo set for your Light Bowgun.
  • Select Ammo + or : Select usable ammo from the ammo you possess.

Rapid Fire Mode

Rapid Fire allows you to fire certain kinds of ammo quickly. After firing, press to chain into a Rapid Chaser Shot — a quick-fire attack that ends with a particularly powerful shot.

After fiiring, you can also chain into a Burst Step with + (or + + in Focus Mode) and fire while in motion. If no ammo is loaded, you will instead use Sidestep Reload.

  • Aim/Focus
    • Fire
    • Rapid Chaser Shot ( After Firing)
    • Burst Step ( + after firing)
  • Switch Modes: Switch your current mode to Standard Mode.
