Charge Blade

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Cut Damage

Charge Blade (チャージアックス chyaaji akkusu, "charge axe") is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Charge Blade is both a sword and an axe, bringing the best of those two weapons in one. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Charge Blade Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • The raw damage for Axe: Element Discharge I and II has been increased.
  • The raw damage for multi-hit attacks while in Power Axe Mode has been increased.
  • Adjusted Axe: Element Discharge I and II so that, when an enhanced phial is used during Power Axe Mode, the explosion damage properly reflects the enhanced phial's effect.

Morph between the more agile Sword Mode for maneuvering, then switch to the more powerful Axe Mode to lay down some serious damage.

MHWilds Charge Blade Patch Changes

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • Guard points now have Perfect Guard detection, and you can now chain into Savage Axe Slash upon a successful Perfect Guard from a guard point.
  • Explosive phial raw damage from chaining into Super Amped Element Discharge from certain attacks (such as Amped Element Discharge) has been increased.
  • Raw damage for Amped Element Discharge and Super Amped Element Discharge has been increased.
  • Weak Slash, Return Stroke, and Shield Thrust raw damage have been slightly increased.
  • Explosive phial raw damage for Axe: Element Discharge, Axe: Amped Element Discharge, and Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge have been increased.
  • Impact Phial explosion raw damage has been increased while in Sword Boost Mode.
  • Axe: Element Discharge now chains more quickly into other basic axe moves like Axe: Dash Slam or Axe: Lateral Fade Slash.
  • The raw damage for multi-hit attacks while in Power Axe Mode has been decreased.
  • Fixed an issue where the phial explosion damage boost would not be applied when performing Element Discharge I or similar attacks with an Impact Phial while phials were enhanced.
  • Fixed an issue where sharpness would decrease more than normal when performing a guard point while Elemental Boost was activated.
  • Fixed an issue where the damage scaling from the Rapid Morph skill was being incorrectly applied to certain jumping attacks in axe mode.

Monster Hunter Wilds Charge Blade Changes

  • You can now overcharge the phials in Monster Hunter Wilds for more added damage when using the axe.
  • The number of phials consumed by your attack depends on the move used, as some Axe Mode attacks, like the Super Amped Elemental Discharge attack, empty all of your remaining phials.
  • Even though the power axe mode reduces the energy phial usage by half for attacks, the Super Amped Element Discharge Attack, while you have 3 or more charged phials in axe mode, will still use up all of your charged phials.
  • You can now overcharge the excess energy accumulated with your sword strike to get the overcharged phials to prevent your sword from bouncing off the monster.
  • Savage Axe Mode: Triggering the mode in Monster Hunter Wilds now reduces the phial usage of your axes’ phial-enhanced attacks including your Amped Element Discharge, meaning you can now stay in axe mode effectively twice as long before you have to refill your phials. By timing your attacks with the axe in this mode, you can deal multi-attack hits on the monster. This can be done by holding your attack input the moment your attack strikes the monster. It can be tricky to pull off but with a little practice, you can consistently perform this and get additional damage out of your attacks.
  • Power Axe Mode: The Charge Blade has a few pathways to get into the power axe mode. The most simple way is the mounted attack finisher and landing the finisher attack will give you the power axe mode. The second way is by performing a perfect guard and following up with a Savage Axe Slash, leading you into the power axe mode buff. You can also get the buff after a successful power clash and follow up with a Savage Axe Slash. The last method is through the Focus Strike: Double Rend while in Focus mode. Regardless of which mode you are in, the strike does the same move: with you hitting the wound with a double sword slash and then switching into the axe mode to do an attack similar to a chainsaw move. Successfully landing this attack and destroying the wounds will grant you the power axe mode buff.
  • Shield Boost: The shield boost also received a considerable upgrade as you can charge your shield after any charging strike from the sword during a combo and then holding the charge button, or after charging your phials. This will transfer the energy from the phials to your shield and give you a boost. It also gives you a guardpoint which will greatly increase your defensive capabilities, and give your shield and element boost in one fluid combo.

MHWilds Charge Blade Weapon Traits

  • Axe Mode trades out defense for powerful attacks, good reach, and the phial's bonus. Charged phials can power up different aspects of the weapon.
  • Sword Mode allows you to maneuver yourself quickly and accurately. You can also charge phials while in Sword Mode to power up Axe Mode.
  • A complex weapon with a sword & shield form and an axe form that both use a phial system.
  • Sword mode boasts mobility and high guard capabilities, while axe mode boasts more powerful attacks. Store up energy while in sword mode, and then use it to deal high damage in axe mode.
  • Morph between the more agile Sword Mode for maneuvering, then switch to the more powerful Axe Mode to lay down some serious damage.
  • Guarding: Yes (Sword Mode)

Charge Blade Tips

  • Don't Over-Charge Sword Energy: Accumulated sword energy is charged into a phial and used, but accumulate too much, and the attack will be repelled.
  • Shield: Elemental Boost: Consume phials to temporarily increase your guard and gain attacks in Axe Mode.

Charge Blade Weapon Properties

  • Sword Mode ↔ Axe Mode: Morphs your weapon, switching modes. When morphing, the shield will automatically Guard attacks while facing forward.
  • Attacks that Block: Some attacks while in Sword Mode can also guard. Timing the move while the shield is facing the incoming attack will let you guard automatically.
    After guarding, you will be able to perform Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge with +.
  • Charge Gauge: Attacking with the sword will store Sword Energy, which can then be used to fill phials when the gauge is yellow or above. Accumulating too much Sword Energy will make your attacks deflect.
  • Element Boost: Enhances your guard during Sword Mode and you will be able to perform Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge.
  • Sword Boost Mode: Your Sword attacks will be enhanced by phial effects, your damage will be boosted, and your weapon will not be deflected.
  • Power Axe Mode: Lessens phials use and allows you to deal damage to multiple places by continuously pressing the button when attacking with the axe. After performing a Perfect Guard, you can use Savage Axe Slash, which will enhance your attacks for a short time.
  • Charged Attack: 'Sword: Charged Attack' and Sword: Condensed Element Slash will lose some effectiveness when overcharged, but are powerful attacks when timed correctly.
  • Phials: Consumed when performing Element Discharge attacks. and explode to cause damage. When enhanced, the phials' effects are even greater.

    • Impact Phial: Adds stun and exhaust, and generates an explosion that pierces through a monster's hide.
    • Element Phial: Increase the weapon's elemental properties, and can inflict large damage via that element.

MHWilds Charge Blade Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • Axe mode allows for strong and deadly attacks.
  • A versatile weapon where you can use the shield, sword, and switch to the axe.

Weapon Damage Types

  • Drastically reduces the movement speed of the hunter.
  • Axe mode significantly slows the hunter's movement.
  • Given its amount of variety, it may be difficult for new players.

Monster Hunter Wilds Charge Blade Controls

Monster Hunter Wilds Charge Blade Controls on Consoles

Sword Controls

  • Sword: Weak Slash : A basic combo starter. You can chain into Return Stroke with , then Roundslash with again.
  • Sword: Charged Double Slash →: Performed by letting go of at the right time. You will perform Rising Slash instead if the timing is off.
  • Sword: Forward Slash +: A lunging slash that moves you forward. You can perform Sword: Shield Thrust after Sword: Forward Slash with +. Use + again to perform Axe: Amped Element Discharge.
  • Guard  : A guard using your shield. Timing your Guard well will allow you to perform a Perfect Guard. Use after a Perfect Guard to perform a Savage Axe Slash.
    • Savage Axe Slash ( after perfect guard)
  • Sword: Morph Slash +: An attack that will morph your weapon from sword to axe.
  • Charge +: Use accumulated Sword Energy to fill your Phials. If the Phials are already full, the surplus will enhance the phial.
  • Shield: Element Boost ( while charging): Spends phials to enter Elemental Boost, which will enhance your Guard and allow you to perform Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge.
  • Sword Boost( while charging): A further boost after Elemental Boost, empowering the sword. While empowered, your sword will not deflect off monsters.
  • Sword: Fade Slash (During a Combo / While in Focus Mode +): You can move in the direction of your choice after each attack during the combo using . You can perform the combo immediately while in Focus Mode.
  • Focus Slash: Double Rend +: A slashing attack effective against wounds. Performs a leaping sword attack from either Sword Mode or Axe Mode. Hitting a wound will put you into Power Axe Mode.

Axe Controls

  • Axe: Rising Slash : A basic combo starter. Press to chain into Axe: Overhead Slash or + to chain into Axe: Lateral Fade Slash. Holding while in Power Axe Mode will allow you to deal multiple instances of damage.
  • Axe: Element Discharge I : A multi-hit attack that consumes a phial. Press to perfom Axe: Elemental Discharge II, then again for Axe: Amped Element Discharge.
    Continually pressing while in Power Axe Mode will allow you to deal multiple instances of damage.
  • Axe: Amped Elemental Discharge +: Amped Elemental Discharge can be chained with into Axe: Amped Element Discharge Follow-Up. Hold + or while in Power Axe Mode to unleash a multi-hit attack that deals massive damage. Press + (With Element Boost) to perform Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge or (while readying Axe: Amped Element Discharge) to perform an Elemental Roundslash, which also activatse Element Boost.
  • Axe: Morph Slash: An attack that will morph your weapon from axe to sword.
  • Focus Slash: Double Rend +: A slashing attack effective against wounds. Performs a leaping sword attack from either Sword Mode or Axe Mode. Hitting a wound will put you into a Power axe Mode.

MHWilds Charge Blade Weapon Combos

Sword: Basic Combo

A combo that allows you to close in from afar using a Sword: Shield Thrust. Using a Sword: Charged Double Slash instead of a Sword: Return Stroke will quickly fill the Charge Gauge.

  • Sword: Forward Slash +
    • Sword: Return Stroke
      • Sword: Roundslash
        • Sword: Shield Thrust +

Element Boost Combo

A combo that allows you to execute a Shield: Element Boost, which can also be performed by charging during any basic combo.

  • Sword: Charged Double Slash' →'
    • Charge +
      • Shield: Element Boost
        • Sword: Shield Thrust +

3+ Phial Elemental Discharge Combo

A combo that chains a Sword: Shield Thrust into an Axe: Amped Element Discharge. The combo ends with an Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge, dealing massive damage.

  • Sword: Shield Thrust +
    • Axe: Amped Element Discharge +
      • Axe: Amped Element Discharge Follow-up
        • Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge +

Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge Combo

After guarding with a guarding attack, you can unleash an Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge. Using Element Boost will reduce the vulnerability window after guarding, making this combo easier to perform.

  • Sword: Morph Slash +
    • Axe: Super Amped Element Discharge + (After Guarding with an Attack that Guards)
