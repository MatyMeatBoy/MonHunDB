Hunting Horn

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Blunt Damage

Hunting Horn (狩猟笛 Shuryōbue, "hunting animal flute") is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Hunting Horn are wielded in pairs and focus more on fast attacks rather than raw damage. Like all Weapons, they have their own unique set of moves, along with an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials you use.

A blunt weapon that excels at providing support via melodies and echo bubbles. Maneuver well to deal damage using the echo bubbles.

Patch Changes of Hunting Horn in MH Wilds

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • Performing Focus Strike: Reverb with precise timing now plays an additional note at the end of the attack.
  • Performing Focus Strike: Reverb with precise timing now sets an Echo Bubble at the end of the attack. (You can prevent the Echo Bubble by holding R2 (PS5) / RT (Xbox) / R key (default, PC) at the end of the action.)
  • Hitting a monster with a single hit of Resounding Melody now triggers a soundwave.
  • Your Seikret no longer stops when performing while mounted.
  • The damage hitbox for Echo Bubble has been significantly increased.
  • The evade window for Offset Melody have been increased.
  • Fixed an issue where switching from one Hunting Horn to another would incorrectly display melodies that had not been played as an Encore.
  • Fixed an issue where connecting with Offset Melody would sometimes fail to successfully offset attacks.
  • Fixed an issue where stocked notes would not properly reset when chaining into a Special Performance.

Changes of Hunting Horn in MH Wilds

  • Echo Bubbles: One of the most notable additions to the Hunting Horn is the introduction of Echo Bubbles. These bubbles provide various buffs to hunters, and a set number of them can be placed down at one time. The specific buffs they offer depend on which Horn is being used. Echo Bubbles can also damage monsters, again depending on the Horn and the notes available, and performing a damaging note while a monster is within range of multiple bubbles can result in a surprisingly high amount of damage.
  • Offset Melody & Special Performance: Another interesting addition to the horn is the Offset Melody. This melody is classified as a Special Performance. What makes this interesting is the fact that you can bank this by playing this similar to other notes but the Special Performance is banked separately and is not consumed when performing an encore with other melodies. Offset Attacks are a new type of attack that was added in Monster Hunter Wilds. These attacks fill up an invisible Offset meter on the monster, much like a status effect, and will topple the monster when you trigger the effect. And like status effects the subsequent triggers will get harder and harder to trigger after the first topple.
  • Resounding Melody & Melody of Life: The Resounding Melody and the Melody of Life. The Resounding Melody is another offensive special performance, though unlike the Offset Melody, it cannot be held. Performing it has the hunter spin the Horn twice while striking the monster, finishing with a slam to the ground that delivers a heavy blow. At the end of the attack, any Echo Bubbles placed beforehand release a damaging pulse that hits any monster within their area. The Melody of Life, on the other hand, is entirely non-offensive. Playing it heals the hunter and any teammates within the surrounding area, including anyone standing inside an Echo Bubble at the time, making it a strong option for a support player looking to heal across a wide area.
  • Performance Beat: You can bank songs after playing specific notes and you can perform the melodies the same as before but now, after each melody is finished playing, you can press the perform button again to do a “performance beat”. The timing for this is strict and will take a while to get over the learning curve but doing this will give you a secondary hit, and if timed perfectly will give you a damage boost. This can be done once per Melody and with being able to bank three melodies, you can stack the damage boost up to three times.
  • Focus Strike: Reverb: The Focus Strike has also been expanded with Reverb. During Focus Mode, the Horn's focus strike draws out and stabs at the wound. Because the move takes a moment to connect and has a fairly limited range, landing it can be difficult. Once it connects, however, it triggers the full Reverb Focus Strike, during which the hunter plays the Horn like a guitar. This introduces another rhythm-based mechanic, allowing a note to be banked with each strum. The timing here is also very strict and takes practice to master. Each note stored increases the damage of the final hit, so storing the maximum of five notes results in the highest possible damage from the move.
  • Echo Waves: Echo Waves also return as a Melody in Monster Hunter Wilds and each Horn will have its specific echo wave and there will be different echo waves for different elemental types.
  • Riding Attacks: The riding attacks on the Seikret are unique for the Hunting Horn as you can fully play and store melody notes or perform them and get the buffs. This is an amazing option to bank notes and keep your buffs' uptime to the maximum.

Weapon Traits of Hunting Horn in MH Wilds

  • Wide variety of team buffs, that can boost chances of succeeding on a hunt.
  • A blunt weapon that allows for repeated stunning.
  • Attacks make notes that can be woven into melodies that give buffs.
  • Melody effects also benefit your allies.
  • Place Echo Bubbles to provide special effects within a designated area.
  • A blunt weapon that excels at providing support via melodies and echo bubbles. Maneuver well to deal damage using the echo bubbles.
  • Guarding: None.
Hunting Horn Tips
  • Note: When performing an attack, a note will play based on your button input
  • Perform: A Melody Effect occurs when certain tones are performed in a sequence.
  • Echo Bubble: When Performing with an Echo Bubble placed, Reverb attacks will emit from the Echo Bubble.
Hunting Horn Properties
  • Echo Bubble: After setting an echo bubble, you will receive a special Melody Effect as long as you are within its area of effect. It will also resonate as you Perform and play notes, dealing damage to any nearby monsters.
  • Offset Melody: A special Perform attack that can Offset monster attacks. Its high damage ensures its usefulness as a normal attack as well.
Hunting Horn Melody Effects
  • Self-Improvement: Increases movement speed. Playing an Encore for this Melody Effect will also raise your attack power and prevent your attacks from being deflected.
  • Attack Up (S) / Attack Up (L): Increases Attack
  • Fire Res (S) / Fire Res (L): Increases fire resistance and prevents fireblight.
  • Echo Wave: A Melody Effect that deals damage. Each hunting horn has different effects. Some deal blunt or slicing damage, while others can inflict elemental damage or status ailments.

Weapon Features of Hunting Horn in MH Wilds

Weapon Advantages

  • Self-improvement is one of the few abilities in the game to have a weapon completely ignore bouncing back, regardless of the hardness of monster parts.
  • Hunting Horns allows Hunters to buff themselves and their allies while doing damage.
  • Hunting Horns possess a longer reach than most melee weapons.
  • Great KO and exhaust potential
  • Sonic wave attacks deal the same damage regardless of the hardness of the part hit.
  • Monster Hunter Wilds also features Elemental waves of each element.
  • You will be loved by other players.

Weapon Disadvantages

  • The Hunting Horn still has a limited set of basic moves/attacks, leaving hunters with fewer possibilities than other weapons.
  • Slow and can get you locked into long animations. This makes the Hunting Horn a hard weapon to master, and not ideal for rookie players.
  • Lower tier DPS compared to other weapons in speedruns
  • Cannot block
