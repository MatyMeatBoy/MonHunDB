Insect Glaive

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Slashing Damage

Insect Glaive (操虫棍 Sōchūkon, "steering insect club") is one of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). What makes the Insect Glaive unique is its connection to a special type of insect known as the Kinsect, which plays a vital role in how the weapon fundamentally works. Like all Weapons, it comes with its own distinct set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used. 


Handle-bugs called Kinsects to collect Extracts to power you up. Use the glaive to vault and perform midair-attacks.

Insect Glaive Latest Patch Notes in MH Wilds

  • Relaxed offset detection, making it easier to trigger offset attacks.
  • The invulnerability window when vaulting backwards has been increased.
  • Increased the distance when vaulting backwards granted by the skill Evade Extender.
  • The raw damage of Strong Reaping Slash and Dodge Slash has been increased.
  • The raw damage and elemental scaling of Strong Double Slash and Tornado Slash have been increased.
  • Fixed an issue that caused the midair combo to have lower than intended attack power.
  • Improved the hit detection of Focus Thrust: Leaping Strike while moving away from the target in the air. This attack will now also retrieve extracts upon a successful hit, as it does when performed on the ground.
  • Fixed an issue where certain parts of Rathalos would not yield the intended extract colors.
  • Fixed an issue where the amount of mount buildup from Jumping Advancing Slash performed as a drawn attack varied depending on the timing of the hit.
  • Fixed an issue where, when quickly inputting commands after getting up, Leaping Slash or Sidestep Slash would trigger instead of Kinsect: Harvest Extract or Kinsect: Recall.
  • Fixed an issue where Descending Slash could not be performed after getting up.
  • Fixed an issue where, when performing a Strong Jumping Slash in Focus Mode and the player took damage while the Kinsect was in flight, the Kinsect would continue to repeatedly hit the monster.

Monster Hunter Wilds Insect Glaive Changes

  • Even though the Insect Glaive lost some of its overall “airborneness” in Monster Hunter Wilds compared to previous iterations, the trade-off is being able to do some impressive damage.
  • Rising Spiral Slash: A new powerful finisher attack that you gain access to when you have all three essences stored. Although it deals a lot of damage, the move will drain all 3 of your essences when you use it, so you’ll have to regather all of them after each use. If you land the attack, the Kinsect flies around you gathering all of the essences in range so most of the time, you can get one or two essences back with the strike, depending on your positioning.
  • Essence Extraction: Received a quality of life upgrade in Monster Hunter Wilds. When you aim your Kinsect, you can now see the essence of the body part you are aiming at, making it much easier to learn where you need to hit for the essence. You can also now charge up your Kinsect, which you can see by the glowing of the base of your Glaive and this will send the Kinsect forward with force piercing through the monster and collecting multiple essences for faster extraction.
  • Red Essence: The red essence has received a small rework in Monster Hunter Wilds. It no longer buffs your attack stat; instead, having a red essence allows you to charge your Descending Slash, which can be charged up to 2 levels with only the red essence. If you have all 3 essences, this move is upgraded into the Strong Descending Slash from which you can combo in the Rising Spiral Slash. White Essence will still increase movement speed and jump height and the orange essence will provide the Flinch Free effect. Green Essence also returns and extracting green essence is a great way to recover some health in the middle of a hunt.
  • Different combination of essences gives different kinds of buffs as well, with red and white increasing attack and white and orange increasing defense, and having all three further increases these buffs. Note that green does not count towards this combination and is used for recovery. With all three essences gathered the Kinsect will now help and attack the monster with the Kinsect spinning around you. It’s a very strong move as with a blunt Kinsect, you can stun monsters more easily now.
  • Focus Thrust: Leaping Strike - While in focus mode, it will perform a thrust and kick back, and when you connect the strike to a Wound, it will shoot the Kinsect out like a grenade popping multiple wounds dealing huge amounts of damage. What makes this move crucial in the Insect Glaive’s arsenal is that after landing this move, the Kinsect will return will all three essences extracted, making it a very efficient way of gathering the extracts and buffs. This can also be done mid-air while you are vaulting, allowing master hunters to keep up the pressure with great repositioning and relentless assaults with the Rising Spiral Slash.
  • Mount Attack: Insect Glaive already boasts a strong mounting capability but with the addition of the Seikret, this capability is enhanced with having access to a Dismount Jumping Attack while mounted from which you can easily mount the monster as well. Mounting attacks received a new finisher move that will have you spin on your glaive on the monster making you look even more cool as you topple it to the ground.
  • The Insect Glaive has undergone a significant shift in playstyle, with a focus now placed on ground-based combat rather than its previous aerial dominance seen in Monster Hunter World: Iceborne. This change was intentionally made to encourage new strategies and playstyles, but it generated substantial feedback from players who cherished the weapon’s iconic airborne combat identity.
  • A key change to the Insect Glaive was the removal of the "Vaulting Dance" move, a signature aerial action that had been a staple in previous games. This decision to exclude the move from the demo was part of an effort to steer players away from relying on aerial combat, directing attention to the updated ground-based mechanics and fresh combat options introduced for the weapon. The developers wanted to encourage players to explore these new mechanics, making the weapon feel more versatile and aligned with the overall design of Monster Hunter Wilds.
  • However, this change did not sit well with many long-time Insect Glaive users, who felt that "Vaulting Dance" was a fundamental aspect of the weapon’s identity. The feedback indicated that the move wasn't just another attack; it was a core part of what made the Insect Glaive unique and enjoyable to use.
  • In response to this, the development team has listened to player feedback and reinstated "Vaulting Dance" in the updated version of the game, adjusting it to complement the new ground-based mechanics rather than overshadow them. This change allows players to enjoy a more balanced and flexible playstyle, where the Insect Glaive’s aerial and ground capabilities can be used together to create more dynamic and engaging combat experiences.
  • With the return of Vaulting Dance, players will be able to combo the move into a variety of aerial actions. The overall Aerial Attack power will also be increased by two levels with each vault of the hunter, strengthening the Aerial Action of Insect Glaives.
  • The execution of the Descending Slash, according to Yuya Tokuda will remain the same. He said that the move would feel tricky at first, but after carefully discussing it with the team members, he made the decision to keep the playstyle as is.
  • Some changes coming to the Insect Glaive will affect the Kinsect extracts. The Orange extract will now negate knockback while the weapon is drawn. The Red + White + Orange extract will also mitigate Wind Pressure, Tremors, and Roars, on top of its current effects.

MHWilds Insect Glaive Weapon Traits

  • The Insect Glaive possesses unparalleled aerial abilities.
  • Rapid, flowing attacks with a good range.
  • Kinsect provides strong self-buffs.
  • The insect glaive can be used to control a Kinsect, a small insect that harvests extracts from monsters to boost your abilities. The insect glaive also allows you to vault, so you can perform jumping attacks anytime.
  • Handle bugs called Kinsects to collect Extracts to power you up. Use the glaive to vault and perform midair attacks.
  • Guarding: None

Insect Glaive Tips

  • Kinsect Combo: When attacking during Focus Mode, your Kinsects will also attack and gather Extracts.
  • Marking and Dust Effects: A Kinsect will automatically attack a marked spot, forming a Dust effect.

Insect Glaive Weapon Properties

  • Kinsect: Insects that fight alongside you by following instructions given to them with your Insect Glaive. Kinsects are able to make a number of Extracts from Monsters that will power you up. You can equip and strengthen a wide variety of Kinsects.
  • Kinsect Combo: Attacking while aiming with during Focus Mode will have your Kinsect attack the monster, collecting Extracts. The Kinsect's trajectory will also change in accordance with your attacks.
  • Dust Effect: Kinsects will leave behind Effect Powder when attacking the marked monster part. Attacking the powder will make it explode, causing a number of different effects.
    • Blast: Build up Blast on the monster.
    • Poison: Build up Poison on the monster
    • Paralysis: Build up Paralysis on the monster
    • Recover: Slightly restore your HP.
  • Extracts: A substance extracted from monsters. There are four kinds in total: Red, White, Orange, and Green. The color you can extract differs depending on the parts of each monster.
    Gathered Extracts can be absorbed by recalling your Kinsect, with each color granting a different effect. Absorbing multiple colors will produce even more effects. The effects will end after a period of time.
  • Extract Effects: Different colors of Extracts will provide different effects. Combine multiple colors to produce even more effects.
    • Red: Allows you to charge with
    • White: Increases movement speed and jump height
    • Orange: Grants Flinch Free
    • Red+White: Increases Attack
    • White+Orange: Increases Defense
    • Red+White+Orange: Alters certain attacks and increases attacks further. Also mitigates Wind Pressure, Tremors, and Roars.

MHWilds Insect Glaive Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • Extremely high mobility paired with superior aerial combat.
  • Ability to vault over low-hitting attacks without expending Wirebug charges.
  • Kinsects provide a great amount of versatility when creating builds.

Weapon Disadvantages

  • Kinsect controls can be difficult to learn and master. Not ideal for new players.
  • Requires quite a bit of resource managemet; You need to learn and memorize which monster parts grant which Kinsect charges, as well as develop a consistency in collecting said charges in order to unlock the weapon's full potential.

Monster Hunter Wilds Insect Glaives Kinsects

Kinsect is a special piece of equipment in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds) for players who choose to wield the Insect Glaive. Kinsects assist hunters by extracting buffs for them while applying debuffs onto Monsters, and since each Kinsect provides different buffs and debuffs, it is important to choose one that pairs well with your playstyle. Like other Equipment in the game, Kinsects can be upgraded by raising them, which improves their attributes. Players can purchase, raise, and manage their Kinsects through the smithy, Gemma.

How to Manage & Raise Kinsects in MHWilds

Before learning how to manage Kinsects, hunters first need to know how to equip them. This can be done by visiting Gemma, who is commonly found at the smithy station in any Base Camp, and selecting "Change Equipment" from the Equipment Menu. From there, selecting the Insect Glaive brings up both your Glaives and your Kinsects, allowing you to choose the Kinsect you wish to equip and confirm the selection. Once equipped, a small yellow checkmark appears in the lower right corner of that Kinsect.When it comes to managing and raising Kinsects, players can purchase the Culldrone and Mauldrone from Gemma. The Culldrone uses a severing attack type and helps recover health, while the Mauldrone uses a blunt attack type and helps poison the monster being hunted. After purchasing a Kinsect, players can access the Manage Kinsects menu, where Nurture Effects display the Kinsect's stats, allowing hunters to choose the option that best fits their playstyle. Raising a Kinsect requires the appropriate Nurturing Materials, which are obtained by going on hunts and collecting monster parts and other materials along the way. The different Nurture Effects offered by each Kinsect, along with the Culldrone Kinsect Tree and Mauldrone Kinsect Tree, are detailed further below.

How to Use Kinsects in MHWilds

When equipped, the Kinsect rests on your character's free hand. During combat, it assists by attacking the Monster, stealing essences, and returning to grant you a buff based on what it collected. Hunters can also command their Kinsect directly by entering focus mode. Once in focus mode, a diamond appears just below the crosshair, displaying different colors. Pressing the Triangle button, the Y button, or LMB launches the Kinsect toward the monster to extract the buff matching the diamond's color. After the Kinsect deals damage, pressing the Circle button, the B button, or RMB recalls it and grants the collected buff.There is also an alternative way to gather buffs while in focus mode. Holding down the Triangle button, the Y button, or LMB until a sound cue plays, then releasing it, launches the Kinsect toward the monster and allows it to land three consecutive blows. Whatever buff the Kinsect collects can be tracked through the beetle icon located below the health and stamina bars in the upper left corner of the screen.

How to Obtain More Kinsects in MHWilds

You can purchase the two base Kinsects, Culldrone and Mauldrone from Gemma. From there, you will need to progress through the story to unlock the feature to raise your Kinsects.

For Rarity 2 Kinsects, you will need to progress through the Chapter 2 Main Missions by defeating Rey Dau in the mission The Eye of the Storm. Go to Gemma to raise your Kinsect given that you have the required Nurturing Materials to do so.

Rarity 4 Kinsects can be unlocked by capturing a Yian Kut-Ku in the Chapter 4 Main Mission New Ecosystems. This unlocks High Rank and also raising your Kinsects to be rarity 4 Kinsects, allowing you to be able to obtain all 20 Kinsects.

Kinsect Differences in MHWilds

The Insect Glaive is one of the more unique weapons in MHWilds and still carries over the same aeriel playstyle it has from the other games, just without its overall "airborness" as the long aerial combos have been taken away. Fortunately, the damage dealt by the Insect Glaive has increased significantly.

Moveset Changes

Rising Spiral Slash is a new finisher introduced in Monster Hunter Wilds. Once all three essences have been filled, hunters can perform this powerful attack, which drains all three essences at once. Interestingly, the Kinsect continues to collect essences in range while the attack is performed, meaning hunters will typically receive at least one or two essences back even after using them up.

Essence Extraction has also been improved. When aiming the Kinsect, hunters can now see which essence will be collected based on the specific body part being targeted, making it easier and more efficient to gather the type of essence needed.

Red Essence no longer buffs the attack stat as it once did. Instead, it allows hunters to charge the Descending Slash, which can now be charged up to two levels. Once all three essences are collected, the Descending Slash is upgraded into the Strong Descending Slash, which can then be comboed directly into the Rising Spiral Slash.

White Essence, Orange Essence, and Green essence still remain the same.

  • White Essence: Increases movement speed & jump height
  • Orange Essence: Provides Flinch Free effect
  • Green Essence: Recovers health gradually

Combining the Essences will also provide different buffs such as the following:

  • Red & White: Increases attack
  • White & Orage: Increases defence

Once all three essences have been collected, your Kinsect will aid you in combat by attacking the Monster.

Focus Thrust: Leaping Strike is a new attack where you can perform a thrust and kick back while in focus mode. Connect the strike to a wound to shoot out the Kinsect that will cause explosions on the wound to deal large amounts of damage. After landing the move, your Kinsect will return to you with all three essences extracted.

Mount Attack is an attack you can perform while mounted on your Seikret, as well as a dismount jumping attack which allows you to easily mount the monsters.

Kinsect Nurturing Effects in MHWilds

As mentioned above, Nurturing Effects are your Kinsects' Stats. You can find each Kinsect's attack type, powder type, and bonus. The Kinsect's attack type will either be Blunt or Severing. Blunt attacks can exhaust or stun Monsters more effectively, especially when attacked on the head, meanwhile, severing attacks work effectively against monster parts that can be cut off, such as Monster tails.

Kinsect Powder is the type of powder your Kinsect creates during combat. Blast, Poison, and Paralysis powder types are ailments that can be inflicted on Monsters and will accumulate over time. For example, if there is enough build-up of Poison powder, the Monster will receive poison damage over time. The Recover powder type helps you recover your health over time.

Monster Hunter Wilds Insect Glaive Controls on Consoles

  • Rising Slash Combo : Press after Rising Slash Combo to perform Reaping Slash. Pressing again after that will perform Double Slash. You can perform an attack while moving by holding in your desired direction during Rising Slash Combo or Reaping Slash.
  • Wide Sweep : A sweeping attack that covers a wide area. It will also recall Kinsects if used while in Focus Mode.
  • Kinsect Harvest Extract+ : Commands your Kinsect to rush forward and gather an Extract from the part of the monster it hits. The kinsect will fly towards the reticle that can be displayed with .
  • Charge +: will charge your Kinsect's attack, allowing them to gather Extract from each part of the monster that it passes through.
    • Kinsect Boost+
  • Kinsect: Recall +- Orders the Kinsect to return to you. The Extracts it brings back will strengthen you.
  • Kinsect: Mark Target : An attack that creates a mark for your Kinsect to follow. Your Kinsect will attack the marked body part, leaving behind a Dust Effect.
  • Kinsect: Fire + : A long-range attack that creates a mark for your kinsect to follow. Your Kinsect will attack the marked body part, leaving behind a Dust Effect.
  • Vault +: You can perform a number of different actions after vaulting. Vaulting backwards will make you temporarily invulnerable. Pressing while in the air will perform an attack that moves you a large distance forward, while will perform an attack as you fall back to the ground.
    • Jumping Advancing Slash ( while Jumping)
    • Jumping Slash ( while jumping)
    • Midair Evade ( while jumping)
  • Focus Thrust: Leaping Strike +: A thrusting attack that is effective against wounds. Successfully hiiting a wound or weak point will cause your Kinsect to pierce through the monster and collect Red, White, and Orange Extracts.
  • Charge ( with Red Extract): A chargeable move when you have a Red Extract. After charging, use for Descending Slash while on the ground, or Descending Thrust while in the air. Preserve your charge and chain in different attacks with . If you have three Extracts, you can use a powerful finisher attack after the charge attack with + . This will end the enhanced state, but you will be able to perform a powerful attacks and collect Extracts with your Kinsect.
    • Descending Slash .
      • Rising Spiral Slash + with Three Extracts.

MHWilds Insect Glaive Weapon Combos

Basic Combo

A combo that can perform a Kinsect Combo after every action. All attacks until Reaping Slash can be direction attacks.

You can also move backwards while attacking with Dodge Slash instead of Overhead Smash.

  • Rising Slash Combo
    • Reaping Slash
      • Double Slash
        • Overhead Smash

Kinsect Powder Combo

A combo that involves marking a target for your Kinsect to attack, evading backwards, then advancing forwards in order to detonate the powder your Kinsect left behind.

Can be followed up by another attack.

  • Kinsect: Mark Target
    • Vault (Backward) ++
      • Jumping Advancing Slash

Midair Combo

A combo that allows you to move a large distance in the air.

You can also chain into a Descending Thrust if you have a Red Extract.

  • Vault +
    • Midair Evade
      • Jumping Advance Slash

Powerful Combo with 3 Extracts

A powerful combo that consumes all three Extracts.

You can pair a Kinsect Combo with a Leaping Slash up to the Tornado Slash portion of the combo.

  • Leaping Slash
    • Tornado Slash
      • Strong Descending Slash
        • Rising Spiral Slash +
