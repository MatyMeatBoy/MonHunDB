Long Sword

Weapon Type Melee
Damage Type Cut Damage

Long Sword (太刀 tachi) is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Long Sword is a technical weapon that takes some skill to use properly. Like all Weapons, it has its own unique set of moves, along with an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials you use.

Excels at swift movements and combos. Turn the Spirit Gauge red to transform some moves for more power, and read a monster's moves to counterattack.

Patch Changes of Long Swords in MH Wilds

MH Wilds Hotfix Ver.1.020.00.00 - Title Update 2 (Release Date: June 30, 2025)

  • Elemental scaling for Crimson Slash II has been significantly increased.
  • Raw damage and elemental/ailment scaling for Crimson Slash III has been increased.
  • Crimson Slash III now chains faster into Spirit Blade I.
  • Spirit Release Slash raw damage has been slightly increased.
  • Spirit Roundslash raw damage has been increased when the Spirit Gauge is red.
  • Spinning Crimson Slash raw damage has been increased.
  • Iai Spirit Slash raw damage has been increased when the Spirit Gauge is white and yellow.
  • Elemental/ailment scaling for Crimson Slash I has been decreased.

Changes of Long Swords in MH Wilds

  • In Monster Hunter Wilds, the core mechanic of the red gauge has been changed. You will no longer consume the gauge with your moves but the gauge is now on a timer which can be topped up by some of the special moves that can be performed while using the Long Sword.
  • Focus Strike: Unbound Thrust: With the addition of Focus Mode in Monster Hunter Wilds, the Long Sword has the Focus Strike: Unbound Thrust which bursts wounds on Monsters and deals huge amounts of damage, and landing a Focus Strike levels up your spirit level.
  • Foresight Slash: The foresight slash can be used mid-combo and excels at dodging attacks because it provides the hunter with a window of invulnerability. Even though the Foresight Slash costs a spirit gauge meter, if you land the attack after evading the monster’s attack, it is an excellent counter-attacking option to sustain your damage levels at a high spirit level.
  • Special Sheathe: The Special Sheath allows the hunters to perform the Iai Slash attack and the Iai Spirit Slash attacks. Landing the Iai slash will fill your spirit gauge by a certain amount. The Iai spirit slash is an attack that can lead to the most powerful combo extender available to the Long Sword hunter as it can be followed by a spirit thrust. The Iai spirit slash is also special in that it can increase your spirit level if you land it while the monster is attacking so if you can get the timing right, it is a very powerful tool to level up your spirit gauge fast.
  • Spirit Thrust & Spirit Helm Breaker: The spirit thrust propels the hunter forward with a thrust attack and when you land the attack with any spirit level, it will allow you to chain into the powerful finisher the Spirit Helm, which is a powerful jumping attack that ends with multiple hits to the monster. Landing this attack causes your spirit level to go down by one level.
  • Spirit Release Slash: When you perform Spirit Thrust into Spirit Helm Breaker at the red spirit level, you can follow up with Spirit Release Slash which is a very strong spinning move combo finisher and new addition in Monster Hunter Wilds. The Spirit Release Slash also provides hunters with an uninterruptible hyper armor so if you can execute this move, you will not be knocked out of it during the attack. Landing the Spirit Release Slash refills the Spirit Gauge but missing it will cost you since the attack drains an additional spirit level. Using the Spirit Release Slash will consume two spirit levels in contrast to the one level from using the helm breaker only.
  • Foresight Whirl Slash: Reaching the red spirit level changes your Foresight Slash into a Foresight Whirl Slash, which is a more powerful form where the hunter will perform a spinning jump, providing a longer hyper armor invulnerability window. Connecting this attack will also refill your spirit gauge slightly extending the time of your red spirit level.
  • Spinning Crimson Slash: A new addition for the red spirit level is the combo extenders for your spirit blade combos. In the red state, the third spirit slash can be followed by a Spinning Crimson Slash. The direction of movement during the attack can be controlled and landing this attack also refills your spirit gauge slightly. This addition is also a powerful move because the spinning crimson slash can directly lead into Spirit Sheath and Iai slashes or spirit thrust.
  • Spirit Charge: Another addition is the spirit charge. At any point during your combos, you can hold your spirit blade much like charging up the Great Sword or Hammer to increase your spirit blade attacks The special mechanic about this charge is that you can interrupt the charging at various levels and continue your spirit blade from corresponding levels to lead into the Spirit Roundslash. This spirit charge ability provides more versatility to the already flexible Long Sword move set.
  • Seikret/Mount Attacks: For the Long Sword, the Seikret offers a mounted attack option and a dismount attack option which will propel you off the Seikret in the direction of your choice and can be followed with a jumping attack. There is also a dismount spirit blade which directly leads you into Spirit Blade 3 and the Spirit Roundslash to quickly increase your spirit level.

Traits of Long Swords in MH Wilds

  • A weapon that excels at swift movements and combos.
  • You can effectively counter incoming attacks with a sharp eye and precise timing.
  • Able to roll out of most attack chains.
  • A well-rounded set of moves with good reach and vertical ability.
  • Landing certain attacks charges up the level of the Spirit Gauge, which increases attack damage and unlocks Spirit Blade attacks.
  • When the Spirit Gauge turns red, certain attacks will have increased damage.
  • Excels at swift movements and combos. Turn the Spirit Gauge red to transform some moves for more power, and read a monster's moves to counterattack.
  • Guarding: None
Long Sword Tips
  • Spirit Gauge: The Spirit Gauge fills as you land attacks, enabling powerful Spirit Blade attacks.
  • Spirit Gauge Colors: Landing Special Attacks such as Spirit Roundslash raises the Spirit Gauge's color and increases attack power.

Long Sword Properties

  • Spirit Gauge: Landing attacks on monsters fills the Spirit Gauge. Using Spirit Blade attacks will consume the gauge and landing a Spirit Roundslash or Focus Strike Will raise the gauge one level. When the Spirit Gauge turns red, it's at its maximum level, enhancing some attacks and allowing you to perform powerful follow-up attacks. Landing a Spirit Roundslash or a Spinning Crimson Slash will extend the duration the gauge stays red.

Features of Long Swords in MH Wilds

Weapon Advantages

  • Great weapon to be used for countering a Monster's attack.
  • Triggering the Spirit Blade will make the weapon and attacks stronger.
  • The Long Sword has great reach, considering it to be a melee weapon. Ideal for players that don't want to be always up-close to the Monsters

Weapon Damage Types

  • The movement of the Long Sword has average mobility.
