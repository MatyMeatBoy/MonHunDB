Heavy Bowgun

Weapon Type Ranged
Damage Type Shot (Ammo) Damage

Heavy Bowgun (ヘビィボウガン hebi bougan, "heavy bowgun",重弩) is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Heavy Bowgun is a devastating-ranged weapon that posses a lighter variant, aptly named Light Bowgun. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Heavy Bowgun Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Wyvernblast Ignition and Wyverncounter Ignition raw damage have been increased.
  • The scaling for Wyvernblast Ignition and Wyverncounter Ignition during Ignition Mode ST I and ST II has been increased.
  • Increased the recovery speed of the Ignition Gauge when using Exhaust Ammo, Poison Ammo, Paralysis Ammo, or Sleep Ammo.
  • Adjusted the hitbox of Focus Blast: Wyvern Howl to make it easier to land.
  • Chaining into Guard after firing Spread Ammo is now faster.
  • The amount of Ignition Gauge recovered while using Pierce Ammo has been increased.

Has poweful long-distance, shot-based attacks. Expend energy in Ignition Mode to fire special ammo. Low mobility but offers defense.

Monster Hunter Wilds Heavy Bowgun Changes

  • The changes the Heavy Bowgun received in Wilds will make you feel more like a slow lumbering tank rather than a stationary turret as it received a ton of changes.
  • Heavy Bowgun Mods & Special Ammo: In Monster Hunter Wilds, there are now two customizable categories for each Bowgun, Mods and Special Ammo slots. The Heavy Bowgun will have 2 slots for Mods and 2 slots for Special Ammo.
  • The mod slots will allow you to choose which ammo type you are using and will have more shots per magazine. Another thing to note is that the two mod slots will offer different Magazines and mods. Generally, the upper one is exclusive to magazines and the lower one has access to Upgrades.
  • For the Magazines, you can choose between Normal, Pierce, and Spread base ammo magazines or the Elemental Boost magazine. For the Upgrades, you can choose between the Standard Mode Upgrade and the Ignition Mode. The Standard Mode Upgrade will increase your Bowgun's damage per shot in the Normal mode for all the ammo types depending on the current level of your Bowgun. The Ignition Mode will increase your Ignition Gauge Recovery level, and increase the rate at which you regain your Ignition Gauge.
  • For the second shot option in Ignition Mode, the Heavy Bowgun can choose between the defensive Wyvern Counter Ignition or the more offensive Wyvernblast Ignition. The Wyverncounter Ignition unleashes a powerful counterattack that deals massive damage. The Wyvernblast ignition turns your Heavy Bowgun into a grenade launcher and launches a mid-range attack that hits a wide area.
  • The Heavy Bowgun also received a new mode like the light counterpart and this mode is the Ignition Mode. Going into ignition mode allows you access to two different firing types and using these shots will drain the meter and you will need to switch back into normal mode when the gauge is empty. The gauge will refill back normally over time or when you land your shots.
  • Wyvernheart Ignition & Wyvernpiercer Ignition: This will be specific to the special ammo you have equipped on the Bowgun and you can mix and match these to suit your playstyle. You will be able to choose between two options for each firing mode. The Wyvernheart Ignition turns your Heavy Bowgun into a literal heavy machine gun, while Wyvernpiercer Ignition shoots a slow-moving single bullet that pierces through the monster for a time and it will get stronger as it goes increasing the damage, the more it passes through the monster.
  • Wyvern Counter: For the second mode, you can choose between the Wyvern Counter and the Wyvern Blast. The Wyvern Counter is an entirely new move added to the Heavy Bowgun. What this move does is, it will attach a device to the barrel of your Bowgun and begin charging and when detonated, it deals a massive amount of damage that will build up the new Offset status on a Monster. It can also be charged to do more damage but the move will go off by itself after a certain amount of time which is relatively long.
  • Wyvern Blast: The Wyvern Blast is the other option available in the slot for the Heavy Bowgun. When you fire off this attack, you will kneel, but won’t have to hold the inputs. You will fire off two cluster grenades that split off into 3 small grenades each upon pressing the button again. This is a mid-range attack that covers a wide area and will deal considerable damage if all six of the grenades hit the target. This is a purely offensive option and will take away your ability to deal Offset damage using the Heavy Bowgun.
  • Ammo: The Heavy Bowgun has also benefitted immensely from the changes made to the ammo system. Even though there are no new ammo types added to the game, some of the ammo now functions differently when compared to the previous games in the franchise. The Heavy Bowgun shares the changes to ammo with the Light Bowgun with the normal ammo is now the rapid-fire variant from the previous iterations which is completely new for the Heavy Bowgun and both the spread and piercing shots now have unlimited ammo.
  • Focus Blast: Wyvern Howl - The Focus Blast for the Heavy Bowgun is known as the Wyvern Howl and it will fire a missile that will stick in the monster before blowing up similar to the sticky ammo, if you hit a Wound with the shot.
  • Even though there are no special riding attacks with the Seikret for the Heavy Bowgun, it is special in that you can use all the ammo types while you are mounted and attack the monster for even more mobility although you cannot use the Ignition Mode while you are mounted. The Seikret also offers an excellent option for mounting with the ability to perform a jumping reload.

MHWilds Heavy Bowgun Weapon Traits

  • Specializes in high damage, and explosive ammo.
  • In MH: Wilds, you no longer need to expend resources for base damage sources (normal, pierce, and spread ammo). This means you don't need to worry about replenishing your supplies when you load into different areas (As long as the gauge is not empty). However, for elemental ammo, you still need to expend your resources, to encourage players to plan ahead and manage their supplies in hunting.
  • The Bowgun can be used to attack monsters from long range. There are two types of bowgun: the heavy bowgun, which can use powerful ammo, and the light bowgun, which allows for greater mobility evasive capabilities, and faster shooting.
  • Ammunition comes in every elemental and status damage type, as well as ammo suitable for short or long-range. Ramp-up Skills can further add more ammo types to your bowgun. Always check the ammo capabilities of your bowgun before building!
  • You can expend energy in Ignition Mode to fire special ammo.
  • Energy regenerates over time and when you successfully land hits.
  • On top of the Auto-Guard feature, you have the option to manually block incoming attacks.
  • Has powerful long-distancem shot-based attacks. Expend energy in Ignition Mode to fire special ammo. Low mobility but offers defense.
  • Guarding: Yes (Auto-guard in some stances)

Heavy Bowgun Tips

  • Ignition Mode: Special Ammo can be used during Ignition. Mode depending on the weapon.
  • Fire Normal Ammo: Normal ammo is effective against monsters at close or medium-range.
  • Fire Pierce Ammo: Pierce ammo is effective against monsters at a great distance.
  • Fire Spread Ammo: Spread Ammo is effective against monsters at close or close-range.
  • Wait for Ammo to Auto-Recover: To fire Focus Blast: Wyvern Howl, wait for ammo to recover automatically.

Heavy Bowgun Weapon Properties

  • Ignition Mode: Fire Special Ammo by consuming energy. Energy will automatically replenish while in Standard Mode, and will also recover whenever you land a shot.
  • Auto-Guard: Automatically guards against an attack from in front of you when performing an action that allows to to Auto-Guard. You cannot use Perfect Guard when Auto Guarding.
Special Ammo
  • Wyvernheart Ignition: Special Ammo that can be used during Ignition Mode. Use to continuously fire, with power increasing as you hit a target.
  • Wyverncounter Ignition: Special Ammo that can be used during Ignition Mode. Unleashes a close-range attack with an offset effect. Press and hold to increase power when firing.
  • Focus Blast: Wyvern Howl: Hitting a monster's wound or weak point will cause a piercing explosion, dealing significant damage. Used ammo will be automatically restored over time.

MHWilds Heavy Bowgun Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • Is a veritable turret, capable of high damage with careful positioning.
  • Ammunition covers every possible damage type, including severing and blunt.
  • Typically larger ammo capacity than the Light Bowgun.
  • Dodges further than most Weapons
  • Can heal and support allies with Recover, Demon, and Armor ammo.

Weapon Damage Types

  • Must supply your own ammunition.
  • Wielding a Heavy Bowgun, leaves hunters open since it possesses slow movement, slow dodge animation, and slow sheathing speed.

Heavy Bowgun Weapon Tree

Monster Hunter Wilds Heavy Bowgun Controls on Consoles

  • Fire : Fires loaded ammo straight ahead. Use Aim to fire in a chosen direction.
  • Aim/Focus : Use to ready your bowgun and more easily aim at your target. Press for an attack that's particularly effective on wounds. Press while in Ignition Mode for a powerful attack.
    • Focus Blast: Wyvern Howl Shot +
  • Reload : Reload the selected ammo. Use to reload ammo in order.
  • Switch Modes : Switch your current firing mode to Ignition Mode, allowing you to fire Special Ammo. Pressing again will switch you back to Standard Mode.
  • Guard +: A guard performed with your Heavy Bowgun. Time it well to perfrom a Perfect Guard, allowing you to use a special backstep. You can also Auto-Guard without inputting anything depending on your stance.
  • Select Ammo + or : Select usable ammo from the ammo you possess.
