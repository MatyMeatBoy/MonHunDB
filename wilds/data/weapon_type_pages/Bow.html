Bow

Weapon Type Ranged
Damage Type Shot (Ammo) Damage

Bow (弓 yumi, an oversized Japanese bow) is one out of the fourteen weapon categories in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds). The Bow is a ranged weapon that offers high mobility for hunters. Like all Weapons, it features a unique set of moves and an upgrade path that branches out depending on the materials used.

NOTE: MHWilds Bow Balance Changes as of Patch 1.021.00.00:

  • Fixed an issue where the arrows from an Arc Shot would disappear if the attack hit the top of the area.

Has a variety of arrow attacks and focuses on mobility. Charging arrows will increase their power. Sidestepping allows you to move away from monster attacks.

Monster Hunter Wilds Bow Changes

  • The Bow has received some of the biggest changes and updates to its mechanics in Monster Hunter Wilds giving an already strong weapon more play styles and options than ever before.
  • Trick Arrow Gauge: The most obvious addition is the Trick Arrow Gauge and this is used to Apply Coating, unlike the previous games, in addition to new special attacks. The Trick Arrow Gauge has three separate sections and whenever you apply a coating, you spend one section to gain 8 coated shots. You will need to reacquire the gauge and reapply the coating after you have spent these shots. The trick arrow gauge can be filled in different ways including attacking, charging your shots, and dodging.
  • Tracer Shot and Tracking Arrows: Another big addition to the Bow is the Tracer Shot and Tracking Arrows. As mentioned before, you can fire a Tracer Arrow as you charge your shot. You can now apply a tracer arrow. While you are charging with the click of a button. Applying the tracer arrow will consume about half of your trick shot gauge per arrow.
  • Discerning Dodge: To do this, you need to time your dodge to the moment the monster hits you, and successfully pulling it off will provide you with iframes as well as a massive amount of stamina and trick shot arrow gauge.
  • Flying Swallow Shot: This shot adds even more mobility for the bow user as you can use it to hop cancel out of a sidestep. While charging in the direction of your choice while shooting out multiple arrows. Having a tracer arrow on the monster will help you sustain your damage while you hop out of harm’s way with this move.
  • Arc Shot: Received some reworks and will no longer deal stun damage to the monster or give buffs. As mentioned above, you can now charge your arc shot to increase the number of arrows it will shoot. It will also leave lingering arrows on the monster which can be blown up using a variety of ways such as the Thousand Dragon Arrows, the Dragon Piercer, and the Focus Shot.
  • Dragon Piercer: Returns in Monster Hunter Wilds with an option to do a faster variant after your normal three shots with the instant Dragon Piercer which takes about half the time to ready up and deals half the damage of a normal Dragon Piercer. The instant Dragon Piercer is also the quickest way to detonate the lingering bombs left behind from Arc Shot.
  • Thousand Dragons Shot: Does not require slinger ammo to power it up anymore and this will shoot a volley of spread arrows. This move has the potential to deal massive amounts of damage especially paired with the lingering bombs and the tracer arrows.
  • Hailstorm: The Focus Strike is called Hailstorm. It's different from other weapons in that in addition to the function of destroying Wounds on monsters, you can use the focus strike to detonate a tracer arrow and all the lingering arrows from Arc Shot to deal massive amounts of damage. Triggering this attack will display a circle on the screen and you will lock onto the tracer. The lingering arrows and the wounds if the monster has any. You will be able to charge the lock-on up to three times for increased damage and you can follow up with a Dragon Piercer to deal the maximum damage during your hunts
  • Another change in MH Wilds Bow is that quick shots and power shots will no longer retain your charge level. You will have to rely more on rapid shots and discerning dodges to quickly level up your charges.
  • Rebalancing the Bow with increased attack power and responsiveness of moves such as normal attacks and Arc Shots, while tonight down the power of Tracer shots.
  • When targeting a monster with Focus Strike, multiple wounds are now locked one one by one with a brief delay in between rather than all at once.

MHWilds Bow Weapon Traits

  • Coatings give useful buffs.
  • Boasts high mobility.
  • Charging your arrows will increase their damage.
  • In MH: Wilds, you no longer need to expend resources for base damage sources (normal, pierce, and spread ammo). This means you don't need to worry about replenishing your supplies when you load into different areas (As long as the gauge is not empty). However, for elemental ammo, you still need to expend your resources, to encourage players to plan ahead and manage their supplies in hunting.
  • Striking a monster with a Tracer will cause arrows to home in on it. Tracers will explode after taking a certain amount of damage or when enough time has passed.
  • Unlimited arrows to hail upon the monsters from afar.
  • Special arrows are capable of hitting one monster several times with one shot.
  • The bow is a mid-range weapon that can perform a variety of attacks. Its charged shots can deal significant damage when aimed at a monster's weak points.
  • Has a variety of arrow attacks and focuses on mobility. Charging arrows will increase their power. Sidestepping allows you to move away from monster attacks.
  • Guarding: None

Bow Tips

  • Charged Shot: The power will increase depending on the charge level. Stamina is consumed while charging.
  • Power Coating: Increases an arrow's power and damage dealt.
  • Close-range Coating: The range of arrows is shortened, but power is greatly increased.

Bow Weapon Properties

  • Trick Arrow Gauge: A gauge required to load Tracers and Coatings. Landing attacks or successfully performing Discerning Dodge will fill the gauge. Loading a Tracer or coating will consume the gauge.
  • Tracer & Tracking Arrows: After shooting a Tracer, subsequent arrows will home in on the Tracer until it explodes.
  • Coating Types: Use coatings to add effects to your arrows.
    • Power Coating: Increases arrow damage.
    • Close-range Coating: Reduces arrow range but increases their damage.
    • Piercing Coating: Allows arrows to pierce.
    • Poison Coating: Arrow inflict poison.
    • Paralysis Coating: Arrows inflict Paralys.
    • Sleep Coating: Arrows inflict sleep.
    • Blast Coating: Arrows inflict blast.
    • Exhaust Coating: Arrows inflict stun/exhaust.

MHWilds Bow Weapon Features

Weapon Advantages

  • Great DPS and great Mobility.
  • Puts distance between Monsters and the hunter
  • Ammo management doesn't exist
  • Great Elemental Damage

Weapon Damage Types

  • At the start of the game, bows may not deal as much damage as the other Weapons.
  • Stamina Hungry Weapon means your Dash Juice supply will run low fast.

Monster Hunter Wilds Bow Controls on Console

  • Charge : Draw your bow to fire arrows. The longer you hold , the higher your charge level becomes, increasing the potency of your attacks. After attacking, press for Power Shot
    • Shoot
  • Aim/Focus : Display aiming reticle in the center of the screen.
  • Quick Shot : An attack that quickly fires arrows over a wide area. Does not consume stamina.
  • Power Shot (During Combo): Unleashes a number of powerful arrows in a wide range. Will carry over your current charge level. After pressing, for Power Volley.
    • Power Volley ''
  • Arc Shot + +: An attack that rains countless arrows down from the sky. Some arrows will become Fuse Arrows, which will linger in the monster. It can be charged up to a maximum of three levels, the range and number of arrows increasing with each charge.
  • Charging Sidestep + : A special quickstep available while aiming. Timing this with a monster's attack will allow you to perform Discerning Dodge, raising your Charge Level, and replenishing your Stamina and Trick Arrow Gauge. Dodging with even more precise timing will greatly recover stamina and Trick Arrow Gauge. Pressing during Charging Sidestep will allow you to perform Flying Swallow Shot in the direction of .
    • Flying Swallow Shot +
  • Dragon Piercer +: A powerful piercing shot. Can trigger Fuse Arrow and Tracer detonations.
  • Instant Dragon Piercer + (During Combo): A quick powerful piercing shot. Can trigger Fuse Arrow and Tracer detonations.
  • Thousand Dragons ++: Fire multiple arrows at once. Can trigger Fuse Arrow and Tracer detonations.
  • Select Coating + or : Select a coating.
  • Apply Coating : Load the selected coating. While loaded, press to unequip the selected coating.
  • Ready Tracer + : Loads a tracer.
  • Focus Fire: Hailstorm' + : An attack that is effective against Wounds. Aim your reticle at a wound or Tracer to launch a tracking arrow that will hit multiple places on the monster. Charge up to level three to increase the amount of arrows and unleash a follow-up attack when hitting a wound.'

Dragon Piercer Combo

A combo that makes use of your charge level to unleash Dragon Piercer.

  • Charged Shot →
    • Power Shot
      • Power Volley
        • Dragon Piercer +

Thousand Dragons Combo

A combo that makes use of your charge level to unleash Thousand Dragons.

  • Charged Shot →
    • Power Shot
      • Power Volley
        • Thousand Dragons ++

Tracer Combo

A combo that unleashes a Tracer at a high charge level. You then perform a Power Shot and Power Volley while maintaining your charge before executing a Charging Sidestep in time with a monster's attack, allowing you to perform a Flying Swallow Shot to put distance between you and your target.

  • Ready Tracer +
    • Power Shot (After Charged Shot)
      • Power Volley
        • Flying Swallow Shot (During Charging Sidestep)

Arc Shot Combo

A combo that starts with unleashing Fuse Arrows with Arc Shot. You then perform Flying Swallow Shot during Charging Sidestep, which puts you in position to fire Dragon Piercer or Thousand Dragons to detonate the lingering Fuse Arrows.

  • Arc Shot + +
    • Flying Swallow Shot + (During Charging Sidestep)
      • Dragon Piercer +
