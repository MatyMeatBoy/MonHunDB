From Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki


Skills, also known as Equipment Skills, in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds) are additional abilities attached to certain items, Weapons, and equipment. This feature was originally introduced in Monster Hunter Rise, though its return to Monster Hunter Wilds was unconfirmed at the time. Wielding equipment with skills can provide buffs, additional effects, or other bonuses, and hunters can choose to equip different weapons and equipment based on the skills they want, allowing them to further tailor their build. This page covers helpful guides and a full list of the available skills in Monster Hunter Wilds.

Group Skills in MH Wilds

Group Skills are a new type of skill debuting in Monster Hunter Wilds. They activate once a certain number of armor pieces sharing the same Group Skill are equipped. This is similar to Set Bonus Skills work, but unlike those skills, Group Skills do not require the armor pieces to come from the same set, which gives Builds significantly more versatility.

Equipment Skills in MH Wilds

In Monster Hunter Wilds, Equipment Skills are divided into two distinct categories: Weapon Skills and Armor Skills. Weapon Skills generally influence weapon-specific actions and damage output, while Armor Skills focus more on utility and support, such as Speed Eating or Divine Blessing. This separation allows for more specialized builds tailored to individual playstyles.

The system also brings changes to decoration slots, as Decorations for skills are now tied to their corresponding category. Weapon Skills can only be slotted into weapons, while Armor Skills can only be slotted into armor. This structure creates a clearer distinction between the roles weapons and armor play in building a loadout, and encourages hunters to think more strategically about how they equip their gear.

Skill Adjustments in MH Wilds

Skill level caps have been rebalanced to promote variety and prevent hunters from over-relying on certain abilities. Skills that were considered overpowered have had their caps reduced, while others have seen their caps increased to encourage experimentation and bring more attention to previously overlooked abilities. These adjustments are meant to create a more dynamic and balanced hunting experience overall.

Set Bonus Skills in MH Wilds

Set Bonus Skills are a type of skills in Monster Hunter Wilds that are activated once a determined amount of Armor Pieces of the same Armor Set are equipped. These types of Skills are associated to the Armor Set, not only aesthetically or thematically, but also in terms of functionality. Set Bonus Skills can also provide stronger bonuses the more Armor Set pieces are equipped at the same time.

Weapon and Armor Skills Integration in MH Wilds

In previous entries, only armor came with built-in Equipment Skills. When those skills happened to be tied to a specific weapon type, it often forced players to match their armor choice to that particular weapon, which limited flexibility. This could discourage experimentation with different playstyles or weapon types, since armor sets with unique skills became less viable outside of their intended use.

By introducing Weapon Skills as part of the new system, Monster Hunter Wilds resolves this issue, giving players greater freedom to mix and match their gear while still maintaining effective builds.
