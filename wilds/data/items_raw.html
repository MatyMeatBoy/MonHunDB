Items | Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki

This page was last edited on 23 April 2026, at 08:32.
From Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki


Items in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds) encompass all the items and objects that can be carried and used by Hunters and would provide additional effects, aid, recovery, temporary buffs, or used for crafting and other uses. These are typically carried in the Item Pouch which can hold a limited amount of items. They are then usually stored in a Supply Box when they are not in use. Items are sorted into different categories depending on their type or use. Consumables are a common type of item that has limited or one use. Different items can be sourced or obtained as loot, as Monster drops, or earned with progress or different interactions as rewards.
