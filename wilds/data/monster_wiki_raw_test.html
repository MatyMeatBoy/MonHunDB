Gameplay Information

The following information is for Monster Hunter Wilds. The Gameplay Information for other titles can be found from the navigation bar at the top of this page.

Rathalos is found in the Scarlet Forest and Oilwell Basin, with Tempered individuals also being found in the Ruins of Wyveria. It can also appear in the Wounded Hollow. In the Forest, he will most commonly appear during the Fallow, while in the Basin, he emerges most often during the Inclemency and more rarely in the Fallow.

Rathalos, while grounded, primarily attack by biting and shooting fireballs. However, Rathalos prefers to attack from the sky, lunging at the hunter with his poisonous talons and breathing out streams of fire. Damaging him enough while he is flying causes him to flinch in the air for an extended period of time, flinching him again will knock him out of the sky. Flash Bombs can also be used to instantly knock him out of the sky.

His nest in Area 11 of the Scarlet Forest features piles of decaying plant matter. If one of his flame attacks touches the piles, they will burn for roughly a minute.

After performing any of his fire breathes, Rathalos' mouth is left exposed as a weak point for Focus Strikes. If a Focus Strike is successfully performed on his mouth, Rathalos will reel back and be briefly knocked down, however there is a cool-down to this interaction.

Hunter's Notes

Monster Hunter Wilds
Characteristics: Fearsome male wyverns known as the “Kings of the Skies.” Alongside the Rathian, they stake out a wide territory around their nest, and it is the male’s role to watch from above. Armed with powerful poisonous claws and fiery breath, Rathalos use their aerial prowess to descend on invaders.

Helpful Hints: As Kings of the Skies, Rathalos will attack with a variety of aerial strikes. A bright flash should be enough to disorient them and bring them down. When Rathalos are in trouble, their Rathian mates may swoop in to help, so be sure to have dung pods and large dung pods at the ready.

Map Description: Fearsome wyverns known as "Kings of the Skies." Armed with powerful poison claws, they use their aerial prowess to descend on prey from above.

What does this mean?
  • Threshold - the amount of status buildup required to trigger the status effect. There are 2 values: the initial threshold, which the monster starts a quest with, and the maximum threshold, reached when the status is inflicted enough times. Each time the threshold is met, it resets and must be reached again, but this time at a higher value. How much higher the next threshold is depends on the Increase value.
  • Increase - this is how much the Threshold increases by each time the status effect is triggered, until the maximum threshold is reached.
  • Duration - how long the status effect lasts when triggered, if applicable.
  • Decay - how much the status buildup on the monster decreases by over time, and the time interval it decreases at.
  • Damage - the amount of damage dealt by the status effect when triggered, if applicable. Note that Exhaust "Damage" refers to the amount of stamina the monster loses when the status triggers.

What does this mean?

Trap & Flash Pod Effectiveness:

  • Duration - the amount of time that the monster spends trapped or blinded the first time a trap or flash pod is used on it.
  • Duration (Exhausted) - the amount of time the monster spends trapped or blinded if the first time a trap or flash pod is used on it is while it is exhausted.
  • Tolerance Reduction - how much the trap/flash duration decreases by each successive time the monster is trapped or flashed.
  • Min. Duration - the minimum amount of time a monster can spend trapped or blinded after repeated uses of traps or flash pods.

Other Items:

  • For Meat, a ✓ indicates that the monster will eat raw meat (including drugged meat) placed on the ground while it is exhausted. A ✗ means it will never eat raw meat.
  • For Dung Pods, the effectiveness is the % chance of the monster fleeing on each attempt while it is under the dung pod status effect. This effect lasts 30 seconds, and the first attempt to leave is made as soon as the dung pod first hits the monster. If this attempt is unsuccessful, another attempt is made 5 seconds later and then every 5 seconds after that until either the 30 seconds have expired or a successful attempt occurs, and the monster leaves the area. An ✗ means that monster is unaffected by dung pods.
  • For Sonic Pods, a ✓ indicates that the pod has a special interaction with the monster under certain conditions, which are shown in () parentheses. A ✗ means that the pod does not do anything to the monster outside of its universal effects.
