Piragill

Leviathans whose bodies are covered with beautiful scales and sharp blades. As a pack, they work together to surround prey, but since most Piragill are too timid to make the kill, they usually wait for an opening and lunge all at once. Don't underestimate them; on water, their speed is unmatched, and those blades will make mincemeat of a hunter that wanders too close.
