Gogmazios

A mysterious elder dragon covered head to tail in a viscous black liquid that was found hibernating in an underground expanse beneath Azuz. It has reportedly been raiding weapon stockpiles across the land to slake its need for gunpowder, and during these confrontations various items have adhered to its body. Among these, most remarkably, is a formidable ancient weapon the likes of which has not been seen in ages.
The oil covering Gogmazios's body can be softened with fire attacks, making it easier to land hits and exposing its weakness to the dragon element. Knowing what element to use when is key. Weapon fragments may be visible on some of its body parts—break them to obtain materials. Attacking Gogmazios while it is in flight will break its posture and create openings. Beware the super explosion triggered by its gaseous state fire breath.
