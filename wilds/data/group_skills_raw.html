Group Skills | Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki

This page was last edited on 17 December 2025, at 16:31.
From Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki


Groups Skills in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHW) are a new special type of skills introduced in this new entry. Group Skills work similarly to Set Bonus Skills, but instead of equipping armor pieces of the same set to activate it, you have to equip armor pieces that possess the same Group Skill, even if they belong to different Armor Sets.
