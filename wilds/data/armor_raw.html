From Monster Hunter Wilds Wiki

Armor in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHWilds) is an equipment category that grants defensive properties and changes the appearance of the Hunter. Armor is equipped to improve a player's defense and add specific resistances. Different Armor pieces can usually be collected as a set in the game which are comprised of five pieces, a Head Piece, Chest Piece, Arm Armor, Waist Armor, and Leg Armor. Armor Equipment pieces are obtained individually an equipped into each specific slot on their own. Learn about each available Armor piece and set on this page and how to find each equipment piece.

Monster Hunter Wilds Armor Equipment Slots

Armor in Monster Hunter Wilds can be collected as a set, which can each be equipped onto a designated slot. There are five equipment pieces in a set that can be slotted into the following:
  • Head
  • Chest
  • Arms
  • Waist
  • Legs

Gendered Armor in Monster Hunter Wilds

Gendered Armor has been removed in Monster Hunter Wilds. This means that one of the most community-requested features would finally be implemented. There is no longer a Female or Male Armor, as players will be able to wear and personalize their hunter however they see fit.

Cosmetic Customization & Fashion

Monster Hunter Wilds adds a few options to let you work on your hunter's looks. The new Customization Menu, allows you to toggle visibility for each armor piece, without losing any effect of stats given by the armor, the armor piece will still be worn but just not visible. There's also an option to add 2 colorings for each armor piece, also a single coloring for pants and another for chest clothes.

Armor Sets in Monster Hunter Wilds (MHW) are collections of armor pieces that provide hunters with a complete defensive ensemble. There are five armor slots available: Head Armor, Chest Armor, Arms Armor, Waist Armor, and Legs Armor. Equipping multiple pieces from the same set can boost elemental resistances, offering enhanced protection. Each armor set is crafted using materials from specific Monsters, reflecting the distinctive look and essence of those creatures in its design.


Monster Hunter Wilds All Armor Sets


What are Armor Sets in Mh Wilds?

In MH Wilds, armor sets are full sets of armor made up of the usual five pieces: head, chest, arms, waist, and legs. Equipping multiple pieces from the same set can activate set bonus skills, and some full sets are special because they also change the character’s appearance. Each armor set is crafted using materials obtained from specific Monsters, with its design and appearance reflecting the distinctive traits and essence of the creature from which those materials were gathered.


What do Armor Sets Provide in MH Wilds?

Armor sets in Monster Hunter Wilds do more than provide defense. In addition to their defensive stats, armor pieces also grant skills, with some pieces focusing on supportive effects while others offer more defensive benefits. According to the official manual, equipment skills and armor set bonuses function as separate systems. This means a hunter's build can be strengthened not only through the skills and stats provided by individual armor pieces, but also through additional bonuses that are activated by wearing specific combinations of armor pieces.
